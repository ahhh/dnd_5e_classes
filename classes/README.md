## Picking a class

| Class | In one line |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Artificer](artificer/README.md) | An inventor who infuses mundane objects with magic and treats items as spells. |
| [Barbarian](barbarian/README.md) | A fierce warrior who channels primal rage into devastating melee combat and near-unkillable durability. |
| [Bard](bard/README.md) | A magical performer whose words inspire allies, unravel enemies, and bend reality itself. |
| [Cleric](cleric/README.md) | A divine agent who channels a god's power to heal, smite, and turn back the undead. |
| [Druid](druid/README.md) | A guardian of the natural order who wields primal magic and takes the shape of beasts. |
| [Fighter](fighter/README.md) | A master of weapons and armor who fights more often, more accurately, and longer than anyone. |
| [Monk](monk/README.md) | A martial artist who channels ki into blinding speed, flurries of blows, and stunning strikes. |
| [Paladin](paladin/README.md) | A sworn holy warrior who smites evil with burst radiant damage and shields allies with auras. |
| [Ranger](ranger/README.md) | A wilderness hunter blending martial skill, tracking expertise, and primal magic. |
| [Rogue](rogue/README.md) | A precise, skill-mastering opportunist who turns a single well-placed strike into massive damage. |
| [Sorcerer](sorcerer/README.md) | An innate spellcaster who bends magic itself, reshaping spells with raw sorcerous power. |
| [Warlock](warlock/README.md) | A pact-bound caster with few but always-maxed spell slots, refreshed on every short rest. |
| [Wizard](wizard/README.md) | A scholar of the Weave with the deepest, broadest spell list in the game. |

## Building a character from this repo

1. Skim the one-liners above and open a class page.
2. Follow its **Build a &lt;class&gt;** section top to bottom — abilities, hit points, proficiencies, equipment.
3. Read the **Decisions by level** table so you know what is coming; the biggest one is your subclass.
4. Open the subclass pages linked at the bottom of the class page and pick one.
5. Use the class level table for what you gain each level as you play.

Want the data instead of the prose? Every page is generated from `class.json` / `subclass.json` in the same folder — read those directly, or see the [repo README](../README.md) for the schema.
