Path of the Battlerager

Barbarian Primal Path — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, p. 121

Dwarven spiked-armor specialist who weaponizes their own body and armor.

Battleragers are dwarves who wear spiked armor and hurl themselves into combat, delighting in the chaos of the melee. Most are devoted to the dwarven gods of war and take a grim joy in grappling their foes and impaling them on their own plates.

How it plays. Very narrow — restricted to dwarves by the book's flavor and dependent on spiked armor. Damage output is low compared to other paths, but it is thematic and fun in grapple-heavy builds.

Features

Restriction: Dwarves Only

3rd level

As written in the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, only dwarves may take this path. Many tables waive this restriction.

Battlerager Armor

3rd level

You gain the ability to use spiked armor (a form of medium armor) as a weapon. While wearing spiked armor and raging, you can use a bonus action to make one melee weapon attack with your armor spikes, dealing 1d4 piercing damage plus your Strength modifier. Additionally, when you use the Attack action to grapple a creature, you deal 3 piercing damage to it if the grapple succeeds.

Reckless Abandon

6th level

When you use Reckless Attack while raging, you also gain temporary hit points equal to your Constitution modifier (minimum 1). They vanish when your rage ends.

Battlerager Charge

10th level

You can take the Dash action as a bonus action while raging.

Spiked Retribution

14th level

When a creature within 5 feet of you hits you with a melee attack, the attacker takes 3 piercing damage if you are raging, not incapacitated, and wearing spiked armor.
