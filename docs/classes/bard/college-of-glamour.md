# College of Glamour

*Bard Bard College — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 14*

> Fey-touched charmers who enthrall crowds and command obedience with a word.

The College of Glamour is the province of bards who mastered their craft in the Feywild. Their music and beauty carry an otherworldly power that can dazzle a crowd into adoration or terrify an enemy into submission.

**How it plays.** The premier face and crowd-control bard. Mantle of Inspiration is powerful early-game party mobility plus temporary hit points, and Unbreakable Majesty makes you very hard to attack.

## Features

### Mantle of Inspiration

*3rd level*

As a bonus action, expend a use of Bardic Inspiration to grant temporary hit points to a number of creatures you choose within 60 feet (up to your Charisma modifier). The amount equals twice your bard level. Each creature that gains them can immediately use its reaction to move up to its speed without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Enthralling Performance

*3rd level*

After performing for at least 1 minute, you can charm humanoids that watched and heard you. Each must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (your spell save DC) or be charmed for 1 hour, regarding you as a trusted friend. The effect ends if you or your companions harm the creature. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Mantle of Majesty

*6th level*

As a bonus action, you cast *command* without expending a spell slot and assume a supernaturally majestic presence for 1 minute. During that time you can cast *command* as a bonus action on each of your turns without a slot, and any creature charmed by you automatically fails its saving throw against those castings. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.

### Unbreakable Majesty

*14th level*

As a bonus action, you assume a magically majestic presence for 1 minute. During that time, any creature that tries to attack you must make a Charisma saving throw. On a failure it can't attack you this turn and must choose a new target or lose the attack, and it has disadvantage on saving throws against your spells until the start of your next turn. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.
