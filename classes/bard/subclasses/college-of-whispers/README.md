# College of Whispers

**[Bard](../../README.md) Bard College** · *Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 16*

> Spies and blackmailers who feed on fear and wear the faces of the dead.

Most folk are happy to welcome a bard, but this rare breed uses that goodwill as a weapon. Bards of the College of Whispers gather secrets, terrify with whispered truths, and steal the identities of those they kill.

**How it plays.** An intrigue and infiltration subclass. Psychic Blades adds burst damage, Words of Terror sets up manipulation out of combat, and Mantle of Whispers is a genuinely unique disguise mechanic.

Taken at **3rd level** as your Bard College. It gives you features at **3rd, 6th, 14th level**:

## Features

### Psychic Blades

*3rd level*

When you hit a creature with a weapon attack, you can expend one use of Bardic Inspiration to deal extra psychic damage: 2d6 at 3rd level, 3d6 at 5th, 5d6 at 10th, and 8d6 at 15th. You can do this only once per turn.

### Words of Terror

*3rd level*

If you speak to a humanoid alone for at least 1 minute, you can attempt to seed paranoia. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (your spell save DC) or become frightened of you or another creature of your choice for 1 hour — or 8 hours if it fails by 5 or more. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Mantle of Whispers

*6th level*

When a humanoid dies within 30 feet of you, you can use your reaction to capture its shadow. For the next hour you can use an action to don that shadow as a disguise, appearing as the dead creature was in life and gaining enough of its memories to make Deception checks to impersonate it (with advantage). A creature that suspects you can make a contested Insight check against your Deception. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Shadow Lore

*14th level*

As an action, you whisper a phrase of terrible power to one creature within 30 feet that can hear and understand you. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or be charmed for 8 hours, convinced you know its darkest secret. While charmed it is friendly and obeys your commands, though it won't act suicidally. The effect ends if it takes damage or you give a command that is obviously self-destructive. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
