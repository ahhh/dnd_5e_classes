Druid

A guardian of the natural order who wields primal magic and takes the shape of beasts.

Source: Player's Handbook, p. 64

Druids revere nature above all, gaining their spells and other magical powers either from the force of nature itself or from a nature deity. Many druids pursue a mystic spirituality of transcendent union with nature, and all of them share an understanding that the natural world is a delicate balance that must be preserved — sometimes gently, sometimes with tooth and claw.

Starting Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • a wooden shield or any simple weapon
  • a scimitar or any simple melee weapon
  • Leather armor
  • An explorer's pack
  • A druidic focus

Alternatively, you may start with 2d4 x 10 gp and buy your own gear.

Class Features

Druidic

1st level

You know Druidic, the secret language of druids. You can speak it and use it to leave hidden messages: you and others who know the language automatically spot such a message, and others spot its presence only with a successful DC 15 Perception check — though they can't decipher it without magic.

Spellcasting

1st level

Drawing on the divine essence of nature itself, you can cast spells to shape that essence to your will. You know two cantrips and prepare a number of druid spells each day equal to your Wisdom modifier + your druid level. You can cast any prepared druid spell with the ritual tag as a ritual, and you use a druidic focus as a spellcasting focus.

Wild Shape

2nd level

As an action, you can magically assume the shape of a beast you have seen before. You can use this feature twice, regaining expended uses on a short or long rest.

You can stay in beast form for a number of hours equal to half your druid level (rounded down), then revert unless you spend another use. You revert automatically if you fall unconscious, drop to 0 hit points, or die.

While transformed: you use the beast's statistics but keep your alignment, personality, Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma scores; you retain your skill and saving throw proficiencies plus the beast's; you can't cast spells (though your concentration continues); and your equipment merges into the new form or falls to the ground, your choice.

Druid Circle

2nd level

Choose a circle that shapes your practice of druidcraft. It grants features at 2nd, 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

Wild Companion

2nd level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

You can expend a use of Wild Shape to cast find familiar without material components. The familiar is a fey rather than a beast, and it disappears after a number of hours equal to half your druid level.

Ability Score Improvement

4th level

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

Cantrip Versatility

4th level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one druid cantrip you know with another druid cantrip.

Timeless Body

18th level

Primal magic sustains you. For every 10 years that pass, your body ages only 1 year.

Beast Spells

18th level

You can cast many of your druid spells in any shape you assume using Wild Shape. You can perform the somatic and verbal components of a druid spell while in beast form, but you can't provide material components.

Archdruid

20th level

You can use Wild Shape an unlimited number of times. Additionally, you can ignore the verbal and somatic components of your druid spells, as well as any material components that lack a cost and aren't consumed by the spell — in any form.
