Peace Domain

Cleric Divine Domain — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 31

A bond-weaver who links allies together so they share protection and mobility.

The Peace domain is devoted to the ideals of friendship, reconciliation, and the bonds that hold communities together. Its clerics are peacemakers and mediators who believe the strongest force in the multiverse is people standing together.

How it plays. Regarded as one of the strongest support subclasses in the game. Emboldening Bond hands out a floating d4 to a whole group, and Protective Bond lets allies teleport to each other and take hits for one another.

Features

Implement of Peace

1st level

You gain proficiency in the Insight, Performance, or Persuasion skill (your choice).

Emboldening Bond

1st level

As an action, you choose a number of willing creatures within 30 feet up to your proficiency bonus (this can include you). You create a magical bond among them for 10 minutes or until you use this feature again. While bonded and within 30 feet of one another, each creature can roll a d4 and add it to one attack roll, ability check, or saving throw it makes — once per turn. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Channel Divinity: Balm of Peace

2nd level

As an action, you can move up to your speed without provoking opportunity attacks. Once during this move, you can stop next to a creature and restore hit points to it equal to 2d6 + your Wisdom modifier (minimum 1). You can affect each creature only once, but you may reach several during the movement.

Protective Bond

6th level

Creatures affected by your Emboldening Bond gain the ability to protect one another. When a bonded creature is about to take damage, another bonded creature within 30 feet can use its reaction to teleport to an unoccupied space within 5 feet of the target and take all the damage instead.

Potent Spellcasting

8th level

You add your Wisdom modifier to the damage you deal with any cleric cantrip.

Expansive Bond

17th level

The benefits of Emboldening Bond and Protective Bond now work when the creatures are within 60 feet of each other. Moreover, when a creature uses Protective Bond to take damage for another, it gains resistance to that damage.
