# Order Domain

*Cleric Divine Domain — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 30*

> A priest of law and obedience whose spells provoke allies into free attacks.

The Order domain represents devotion to the laws that govern a society, a fundamental hierarchy, or the cosmic order itself. Its clerics are enforcers, judges, and magistrates who believe obedience must be maintained for civilization to survive. Originally published in *Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica*.

**How it plays.** An outstanding support domain. Voice of Authority hands out a free attack to an ally every time you cast a spell on them, which scales beautifully alongside a martial-heavy party.

## Features

### Bonus Proficiencies

*1st level*

You gain proficiency with heavy armor. You also gain proficiency in Intimidation or Persuasion (your choice).

### Voice of Authority

*1st level*

If you cast a spell of 1st level or higher targeting an ally, that ally can use its reaction immediately after the spell to make one weapon attack against a creature of your choice that you can see. If the spell targets more than one ally, only one of them can make this attack.

### Channel Divinity: Order's Demand

*2nd level*

As an action, each creature of your choice within 30 feet that can see or hear you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure it is charmed by you until the end of your next turn, or until it takes damage. You can also cause a charmed creature to drop what it is holding when it fails the save.

### Embodiment of the Law

*6th level*

If you cast a spell of the enchantment school using a spell slot of 1st level or higher, you can change its casting time from 1 action to 1 bonus action. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum once) per long rest.

### Divine Strike

*8th level*

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 psychic damage. This increases to 2d8 at 14th level.

### Order's Wrath

*17th level*

If you deal your Divine Strike damage to a creature on your turn, you can curse that creature until the start of your next turn. The next time one of your allies hits the cursed creature with an attack, the target also takes 2d8 psychic damage, and the curse ends. You can curse a creature this way only once per turn.
