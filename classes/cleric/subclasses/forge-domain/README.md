# Forge Domain

**[Cleric](../../README.md) Divine Domain** · *Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 18*

> A divine smith who crafts magic items overnight and wears armor blessed to turn blades.

The gods of the forge are patrons of artisans who work with metal — armorers, weaponsmiths, and jewelers. Their clerics see the act of creation as sacred, and the tempering of steel as a metaphor for the tempering of the soul.

**How it plays.** The tankiest cleric. Blessing of the Forge grants a permanent +1 item, Soul of the Forge adds +1 AC on top of heavy armor, and Saint of Forge and Fire gives fire immunity plus resistance to nonmagical weapons.

## Features

### Bonus Proficiencies

*1st level*

You gain proficiency with heavy armor and smith's tools.

### Blessing of the Forge

*1st level*

At the end of a long rest, you can touch one nonmagical object that is a suit of armor or a simple or martial weapon. Until your next long rest or your death, the object becomes a magic item granting a +1 bonus to AC (if armor) or a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls (if a weapon).

### Channel Divinity: Artisan's Blessing

*2nd level*

You conduct an hour-long ritual to create a simple metal item — a weapon, armor, ten pieces of ammunition, a set of tools, or similar — worth no more than 100 gp. You must have metal worth at least equal to the finished item's value, which the ritual consumes.

### Soul of the Forge

*6th level*

You gain resistance to fire damage, and while wearing heavy armor you gain a +1 bonus to AC.

### Divine Strike

*8th level*

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 fire damage. This increases to 2d8 at 14th level.

### Saint of Forge and Fire

*17th level*

You gain immunity to fire damage, and while wearing heavy armor you have resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage from nonmagical attacks.
