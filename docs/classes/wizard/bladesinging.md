# Bladesinging

*Wizard Arcane Tradition — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 76*

> An elven war-mage who dances through battle with a blade in hand and magic on their lips.

Bladesinging is a style of fighting practised by elves. Bladesingers master a fighting style that weaves swordplay, dance, and magic into a single deadly art. In combat, a bladesinger uses a series of intricate, elegant maneuvers that fend off harm and allow them to channel magic into devastating attacks. Originally published in the *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*.

**How it plays.** The best gish in the game. Bladesong adds your Intelligence modifier to AC while granting speed and concentration bonuses, and Extra Attack lets you swap one attack for a cantrip — turning you into a full melee threat with a wizard's spell list.

## Features

### Training in War and Song

*2nd level*

You gain proficiency with light armor and one type of one-handed melee weapon of your choice. You also gain proficiency in the Performance skill if you don't already have it.

### Bladesong

*2nd level*

You can invoke an elven magic called the Bladesong, provided you aren't wearing medium or heavy armor or using a shield. As a bonus action, you start the Bladesong, which lasts for 1 minute. It ends early if you are incapacitated, don these armors, or use two hands to make an attack with a weapon. While your Bladesong is active, you gain:

- A bonus to your AC equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1)
- Your walking speed increases by 10 feet
- You have advantage on Acrobatics checks
- You gain a bonus to any Constitution saving throw you make to maintain concentration on a spell, equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1)

You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses on a short or long rest.

### Extra Attack

*6th level*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. Moreover, you can cast one of your cantrips in place of one of those attacks.

### Song of Defense

*10th level*

You can direct your magic to absorb damage while your Bladesong is active. When you take damage, you can use your reaction to expend one spell slot and reduce that damage to you by an amount equal to five times the slot's level.

### Song of Victory

*14th level*

You can add your Intelligence modifier (minimum +1) to the damage of your melee weapon attacks while your Bladesong is active.
