Way of the Astral Self

Monk Monastic Tradition — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 45

You summon spectral arms, a visage, and finally a full astral body of pure ki.

A monk who follows the Way of the Astral Self believes their body is an illusion. They see their ki as a representation of their true form, an astral self, and their training is aimed at bringing that self into alignment with the physical world.

How it plays. Visually spectacular and mechanically solid. The Arms of the Astral Self give you reach, Wisdom-based attacks, and consistent damage — letting you build almost entirely around Wisdom.

Features

Arms of the Astral Self

3rd level

As a bonus action, you can spend 1 ki point to summon spectral arms of astral energy for 10 minutes. They grant these benefits:

  • You can use your Wisdom modifier in place of Strength for Strength checks and saving throws.
  • When you make an unarmed strike with the arms, you can use your Wisdom modifier in place of Strength or Dexterity for the attack and damage rolls, and the strike deals your Martial Arts die + Wisdom modifier force damage with a reach of 5 extra feet.
  • As a bonus action when the arms are summoned, you can make a special attack against all creatures in a 10-foot cube originating from you.

Visage of the Astral Self

6th level

As a bonus action (or as part of summoning the arms), you can spend 1 ki point to summon the visage of your astral self, which appears as a spectral mask hovering over your face for 10 minutes. It grants:

Astral Sight. You can see normally in darkness, both magical and nonmagical, to a distance of 120 feet.

Wisdom of the Spirit. You have advantage on Insight and Intimidation checks.

Word of the Spirit. When you speak, you can choose to be heard only by a creature of your choice within 600 feet, or by all creatures within 600 feet.

Body of the Astral Self

11th level

When you have both your arms and visage summoned, you gain the body of your astral self, which grants:

Deflect Energy. When you take acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, radiant, or thunder damage, you can use your reaction to reduce it by 1d10 + your Wisdom modifier.

Empowered Arms. Once on each of your turns, you can deal extra damage with one of your Arms of the Astral Self attacks equal to your Martial Arts die.

Awakened Astral Self

17th level

As a bonus action, you can spend 5 ki points to summon your arms, visage, and body all at once for 10 minutes. While awakened, you gain +2 AC, and whenever you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make three attacks with your Arms of the Astral Self instead of two.
