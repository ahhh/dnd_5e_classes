# College of Swords

**[Bard](../../README.md) Bard College** · *Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 15*

> Blade-dancing entertainers who spend inspiration dice on damaging combat flourishes.

Bards of the College of Swords are called blades, and they entertain through daring feats of weapon prowess — sword-swallowing, knife-throwing, mock combats. Many use their showmanship as a cover for far more dangerous work.

**How it plays.** The damage-dealing bard. Blade Flourish adds movement and turns Bardic Inspiration dice into extra damage plus a rider effect, so your dice fuel your own offense rather than allies' rolls.

## Features

### Bonus Proficiencies

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency with medium armor and the scimitar. If you're proficient with a simple or martial melee weapon, you can use it as a spellcasting focus for your bard spells.

### Fighting Style

*3rd level*

Choose Dueling (+2 damage with a single one-handed melee weapon) or Two-Weapon Fighting (add your ability modifier to the off-hand attack's damage).

### Blade Flourish

*3rd level*

Whenever you take the Attack action, your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of the turn. If you hit with a weapon attack, you can expend one use of Bardic Inspiration for one of these flourishes (the die is added to the attack's damage roll in each case):

**Defensive Flourish.** You add the die to your AC until the start of your next turn.

**Slashing Flourish.** You also deal damage equal to the die to one other creature of your choice within 5 feet.

**Mobile Flourish.** You push the target up to 5 feet plus the number rolled, and can then use your reaction to move up to your walking speed to a space within 5 feet of it.

### Extra Attack

*6th level*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Master's Flourish

*14th level*

Whenever you use a Blade Flourish option, you can roll a d6 and use it instead of expending a Bardic Inspiration die.
