# Aberrant Mind

*Sorcerer Sorcerous Origin — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 66*

> An alien influence grants telepathy, free psionic spells, and Subtle Spell for nothing.

An alien influence has wrapped its tendrils around your mind, giving you psionic power. You might trace it to a mind flayer's experiments, a period spent in the Far Realm, or an aberrant ancestor — but something is in your head that was not always there.

**How it plays.** One of the strongest sorcerers. The Psionic Spells list nearly doubles your spells known for free, and Psionic Sorcery lets you cast them with sorcery points *and* apply Subtle Spell at no cost.

## Features

### Psionic Spells

*1st level*

You learn additional spells as you gain sorcerer levels (see the expanded list above). Each counts as a sorcerer spell for you but doesn't count against your number of spells known.

Whenever you gain a sorcerer level, you can replace one spell you gained from this feature with another spell of the same level from the divination or enchantment school.

### Telepathic Speech

*1st level*

You can form a telepathic connection between your mind and another creature's. As a bonus action, choose one creature you can see within 30 feet. You and the chosen creature can speak telepathically with each other while both of you are within a number of miles equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum 1 mile). The connection lasts a number of minutes equal to your sorcerer level, ending early if you use this feature again or are incapacitated.

### Psionic Sorcery

*6th level*

When you cast any spell from your Psionic Spells feature, you can cast it by expending a spell slot as normal, or by spending a number of sorcery points equal to the spell's level.

If you cast it with sorcery points, it requires no verbal or somatic components, and it requires no material components unless they are consumed by the spell or have a cost.

### Psychic Defenses

*6th level*

You gain resistance to psychic damage, and you have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened.

### Revelation in Flesh

*14th level*

You can unleash the aberrant truth hidden within yourself. As a bonus action, you can spend 1 or more sorcery points to magically transform your body for 10 minutes. For each sorcery point spent, you gain one of the following benefits, which visibly alters you: a swimming speed and water breathing; a flying speed and the ability to hover; the ability to see invisible creatures within 60 feet and darkvision out to 60 feet; or the ability to move through any space at least 1 inch wide as though it were difficult terrain.

### Warping Implosion

*18th level*

You can unleash your aberrant power as a wave of similarly aberrant energy. As an action, you can teleport up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. Immediately after you disappear, each creature within 30 feet of the space you left must make a Strength saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 3d10 force damage and is pulled straight toward the space you left, ending in an unoccupied space as close as possible. On a success, it takes half as much damage and isn't pulled.

Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 5 sorcery points.
