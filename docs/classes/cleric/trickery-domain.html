Trickery Domain

Cleric Divine Domain — Player's Handbook, p. 62

A divine trickster who blesses allies with stealth and fights beside an illusory duplicate.

Gods of trickery are mischief-makers and instigators who stand as a constant challenge to the accepted order. Their clerics are a disruptive force, puncturing pride, freeing the oppressed, and mocking tyrants.

How it plays. The stealth and infiltration cleric. Invoke Duplicity is a flexible tool for granting yourself advantage and casting spells from an illusion's position, and Blessing of the Trickster helps the whole party sneak.

Features

Blessing of the Trickster

1st level

You can use your action to touch a willing creature other than yourself to give it advantage on Stealth checks. This blessing lasts for 1 hour or until you use this feature again.

Channel Divinity: Invoke Duplicity

2nd level

As an action, you create a perfect illusion of yourself lasting 1 minute (concentration) in an unoccupied space within 30 feet. As a bonus action on your turn you can move it up to 30 feet, and you can cast spells as though you were in its space. You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature within 5 feet of both you and the illusion.

Channel Divinity: Cloak of Shadows

6th level

As an action, you become invisible until the end of your next turn. You become visible if you attack or cast a spell.

Divine Strike

8th level

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 poison damage. This increases to 2d8 at 14th level.

Improved Duplicity

17th level

You can create up to four duplicates of yourself instead of one when you use Invoke Duplicity, and you can move any number of them with the same bonus action. You have advantage on attack rolls against a creature within 5 feet of any of them.
