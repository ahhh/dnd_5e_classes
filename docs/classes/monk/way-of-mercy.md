# Way of Mercy

*Monk Monastic Tradition — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 44*

> A masked physician-monk whose hands both heal the dying and hasten death.

Monks of the Way of Mercy learn to manipulate the life force of others to bring aid to those in need. They are wandering physicians to the poor and hopeless, and they understand that the same knowledge that mends a body can also end one. They typically wear masks, presenting themselves as neither living nor dead.

**How it plays.** The healer monk — a genuinely unique role. Hand of Healing turns ki into efficient in-combat healing, and Hand of Harm adds solid damage, letting you flex between the two every turn.

## Features

### Implements of Mercy

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency in the Insight and Medicine skills, and you gain proficiency with the herbalism kit. You also gain a special mask, which you typically wear when using your features.

### Hand of Healing

*3rd level*

As an action, you can spend 1 ki point to touch a creature and restore hit points equal to a roll of your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier. When you use your Flurry of Blows, you can replace one of the unarmed strikes with a use of this feature without spending a ki point for the healing.

### Hand of Harm

*3rd level*

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can spend 1 ki point to deal extra necrotic damage equal to one roll of your Martial Arts die + your Wisdom modifier.

### Physician's Touch

*6th level*

You can administer even greater cures. When you use Hand of Healing on a creature, you can also end one disease or one of the following conditions: blinded, deafened, paralyzed, poisoned, or stunned. When you use Hand of Harm, you can also subject the target to the poisoned condition until the end of your next turn.

### Flurry of Healing and Harm

*11th level*

When you use Flurry of Blows, you can now replace each of the unarmed strikes with a use of Hand of Healing, without spending ki points for the healing. In addition, when you use Hand of Harm, you can use it as part of the same attack that triggers it, without spending an extra ki point for the Flurry.

### Hand of Ultimate Mercy

*17th level*

As an action, you can touch the body of a creature that died within the past 24 hours and spend 5 ki points. That creature returns to life with a number of hit points equal to 4d10 + your Wisdom modifier, and is cured of any conditions and poisons or diseases affecting it when it died. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest.
