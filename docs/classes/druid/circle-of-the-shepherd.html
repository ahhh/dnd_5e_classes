Circle of the Shepherd

Druid Druid Circle — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 23

A summoner who calls totem spirits that buff every ally and every summoned creature.

Druids of the Circle of the Shepherd commune with the spirits of nature — especially the spirits of beasts and the fey — and call on them for aid. These druids recognize that all living things play a role in the natural world, and they see themselves as protectors of the voiceless.

How it plays. The best summoner in the game. Spirit Totem buffs your entire team in a large aura, and Mighty Summoner makes every conjured creature substantially tougher and its attacks magical.

Features

Speech of the Woods

2nd level

You gain the ability to converse with beasts: you learn Sylvan, and beasts can understand your speech. You also gain the ability to decipher their noises and behaviour — most beasts lack the intelligence for detailed conversation, but they can convey simple ideas.

Spirit Totem

2nd level

As a bonus action, you summon a spirit that creates a 30-foot-radius incorporeal aura centered on a point within 60 feet, lasting 1 minute. Choose one:

Bear Spirit. You and allies in the aura gain temporary hit points equal to 5 + your druid level when it appears, and have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws.

Hawk Spirit. You can use your reaction to grant advantage on an attack roll made by a creature in the aura, and you and allies have advantage on Perception checks.

Unicorn Spirit. You and allies gain advantage on all ability checks to detect creatures in the aura. If you cast a spell that restores hit points to a creature in the aura, each creature of your choice in the aura also regains hit points equal to your druid level.

You can use this feature once per short or long rest.

Mighty Summoner

6th level

Beasts and fey you conjure with a spell gain two additional hit points per Hit Die, and the damage from their natural weapons is considered magical for overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks.

Guardian Spirit

10th level

Beasts and fey you summon or create with a spell that appear within your Spirit Totem's aura regain hit points equal to half your druid level at the end of each of their turns.

Faithful Summons

14th level

If you are reduced to 0 hit points or are incapacitated against your will, you can immediately gain the benefits of conjure animals as though it were cast with a 9th-level slot. It summons four beasts of your choice of CR 2 or lower, which appear within 20 feet of you and defend you for 1 hour. Once used, you can't use it again until you finish a long rest.
