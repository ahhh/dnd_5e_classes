# Path of the Beast

*Barbarian Primal Path — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 9*

> Your rage grows claws, a bite, or a lashing tail — a beast wearing your skin.

Barbarians who walk the Path of the Beast draw their rage from a bestial spirit within. That power might come from a lycanthropic curse, a pact with a primal entity, or an unusual ancestry — but when the rage comes, so do the fangs.

**How it plays.** Flexible martial damage with a choice made fresh each rage. Tail is the standout — a reaction AC bonus every round. Infectious Fury turns your hits into forced friendly fire.

## Features

### Form of the Beast

*3rd level*

When you enter your rage, you can transform, manifesting one of the following natural weapons. It counts as a simple melee weapon for you, and you add your Strength modifier to attack and damage rolls as normal.

**Bite.** Reach 5 ft, 1d8 piercing. Once per turn when you damage a creature with it, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus — but only if you have half your hit points or fewer.

**Claws.** Reach 5 ft, 1d6 slashing. When you attack with claws as part of the Attack action on your turn, you can make one additional claw attack as part of that action.

**Tail.** Reach 10 ft, 1d8 piercing. When a creature you can see hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to add 1d8 to your AC against that attack, possibly causing it to miss.

### Bestial Soul

*6th level*

Your natural weapons count as magical for overcoming resistance and immunity. You also gain a natural adaptation that you can change after each short or long rest: a swimming speed and the ability to breathe underwater; a climbing speed including difficult surfaces without an ability check; or the ability to jump with a running start as though you had a running start, adding your proficiency bonus in feet.

### Infectious Fury

*10th level*

When you hit a creature with your natural weapons while raging, you can force it to make a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + Constitution modifier). On a failure, choose one: the creature must use its reaction to make a melee attack against another creature of your choice, or it takes 2d12 psychic damage. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Call the Hunt

*14th level*

When you enter your rage, you can choose a number of other willing creatures within 30 feet up to your Constitution modifier (minimum one). Each gains 5 temporary hit points, and while raging you gain 5 temporary hit points per creature that accepted. Once per turn, an affected creature that hits with an attack deals an extra 1d6 damage. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Constitution modifier per long rest.
