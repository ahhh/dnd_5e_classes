Twilight Domain

Cleric Divine Domain — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 34

A guardian of the night who grants 300-foot darkvision and a temp-HP aura every round.

The twilight transition from light into darkness is a time of comfort and rest. The gods of twilight watch over the boundary between day and night, guarding travelers, comforting the dying, and holding back the terrors that come with the dark.

How it plays. Extremely powerful. Eyes of Night gives the party huge darkvision, Vigilant Blessing hands out advantage on initiative, and the Twilight Sanctuary aura delivers temporary hit points or charm/fear removal to everyone, every single round.

Features

Bonus Proficiencies

1st level

You gain proficiency with martial weapons and heavy armor.

Eyes of Night

1st level

You can see through darkness — magical and nonmagical — out to 300 feet. As an action, you can share this darkvision with a number of willing creatures within 10 feet up to your Wisdom modifier (minimum one) for 1 hour. You can share it once per long rest without cost, or by expending a spell slot of 1st level or higher.

Vigilant Blessing

1st level

As an action, you give one creature you touch (including yourself) advantage on the next initiative roll it makes. This benefit ends immediately after the roll, or if you use the feature again.

Channel Divinity: Twilight Sanctuary

2nd level

As an action, you present your holy symbol and create a 30-foot-radius sphere of twilight centered on you, lasting 1 minute or until you are incapacitated or die. The sphere moves with you and is dim light. Whenever a creature (including you) ends its turn in the sphere, you can grant it one of two benefits: temporary hit points equal to 1d6 + your cleric level, or ending one effect causing it to be charmed or frightened.

Steps of Night

6th level

As a bonus action while in dim light or darkness, you can magically gain a flying speed equal to your walking speed for 1 minute. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Divine Strike

8th level

Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon attack, you can cause the attack to deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage. This increases to 2d8 at 14th level.

Twilight Shroud

17th level

The sphere created by your Twilight Sanctuary now also grants half cover to you and your allies inside it.
