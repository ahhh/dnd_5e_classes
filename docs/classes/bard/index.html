Bard

A magical performer whose words inspire allies, unravel enemies, and bend reality itself.

Source: Player's Handbook, p. 51

Whether scholar, skald, or scoundrel, a bard weaves magic through words and music. Bards are the ultimate generalists — capable of filling nearly any role at the table thanks to a spell list that spans healing, control, and illusion, plus a talent for talking their way into and out of almost anything.

Starting Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • a rapier or a longsword or any simple weapon
  • a diplomat's pack or an entertainer's pack
  • a lute or any other musical instrument
  • Leather armor
  • A dagger

Alternatively, you may start with 5d4 x 10 gp and buy your own gear.

Multiclassing

Prerequisite: Charisma 13

Proficiencies gained: Light armor, One skill of your choice, One musical instrument of your choice

Spellcasting: Add your full bard level when determining shared multiclass spell slots.

Class Features

Spellcasting

1st level

You have learned to untangle and reshape the fabric of reality in harmony with your music. You know two cantrips and a growing list of bard spells (see the table). Charisma is your spellcasting ability, and you can use a musical instrument as a spellcasting focus. You can cast any bard spell you know as a ritual if it has the ritual tag.

Bardic Inspiration

1st level

As a bonus action, you can inspire one other creature within 60 feet that can hear you, giving it a Bardic Inspiration die (a d6). Within the next 10 minutes, it can roll that die and add the result to one ability check, attack roll, or saving throw — it can decide to do so after seeing the roll but before knowing the outcome.

You can use this a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum once) per long rest. The die improves to a d8 at 5th level, a d10 at 10th, and a d12 at 15th.

Jack of All Trades

2nd level

You can add half your proficiency bonus, rounded down, to any ability check you make that doesn't already include your proficiency bonus.

Song of Rest

2nd level

If you or any friendly creatures who can hear your performance regain hit points at the end of a short rest by spending Hit Dice, each of those creatures regains an extra 1d6 hit points. The die improves to a d8 at 9th level, a d10 at 13th, and a d12 at 17th.

Magical Inspiration

2nd level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

If a creature has a Bardic Inspiration die from you and casts a spell that restores hit points or deals damage, it can roll that die and either add the number to the healing or add it to one damage roll of the spell.

Bard College

3rd level

Choose a college that shapes your art. It grants features at 3rd, 6th, and 14th level.

Expertise

3rd level

Choose two of your skill proficiencies. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check using either. You choose two more at 10th level.

Ability Score Improvement

4th level

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

Bardic Versatility

4th level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one of your Expertise skills with another skill you're proficient in, or replace one cantrip you learned from this class with another bard cantrip.

Font of Inspiration

5th level

You regain all expended uses of Bardic Inspiration when you finish a short or long rest, rather than only on a long rest.

Countercharm

6th level

As an action, you can start a performance that lasts until the end of your next turn. During it, you and any friendly creatures within 30 feet that can hear you have advantage on saving throws against being frightened or charmed. The performance ends early if you are incapacitated or silenced.

Magical Secrets

10th level

Choose two spells from any class, of a level you can cast. They count as bard spells for you and don't count against the number of bard spells you know. You learn two more at 14th level and two more at 18th.

Superior Inspiration

20th level

When you roll initiative and have no uses of Bardic Inspiration left, you regain one use.
