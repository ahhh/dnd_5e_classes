# Path of the Juggernaut

*Barbarian Primal Path — Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, p. 183*

> An unstoppable engine of forward momentum — you shove, trample, and crash through everything.

Juggernauts are living siege engines. Found among the warriors of Xhorhas and the fighting pits of the Dwendalian Empire, they specialize in overwhelming force: knocking foes flat, driving them backward, and refusing to be moved themselves.

**How it plays.** A control-focused bruiser built around forced movement and prone-locking. Strong in tight quarters and against single big targets, and hard to stop once it gets moving.

## Features

### Thunderous Blows

*3rd level*

While raging, once on each of your turns when you hit a creature with a melee attack, you can force the target to make a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength modifier). On a failure, you push it 5 feet away from you, and you can immediately move into the vacated space without provoking opportunity attacks.

### Spirit of the Mountain

*6th level*

While raging, you cannot be knocked prone or moved against your will by any effect that would push, pull, or slide you.

### Demolishing Might

*10th level*

You deal an extra 1d8 damage to objects and structures. Additionally, while raging, your melee weapon attacks deal an extra 1d8 damage to any creature of the Large size or bigger.

### Unstoppable

*14th level*

While raging, your speed can't be reduced, and you are immune to the frightened, paralyzed, and stunned conditions. If you are affected by one of those conditions when you enter your rage, the effect is suspended for the duration.
