# Battle Master

**[Fighter](../../README.md) Martial Archetype** · *Player's Handbook, p. 73*

> A tactical warrior who spends superiority dice on maneuvers that trip, disarm, and command.

Battle Masters employ martial techniques passed down through generations. To a Battle Master, combat is an academic field, sometimes an art form. They gather knowledge of maneuvers the way scholars gather lore.

**How it plays.** The most tactically rich martial subclass. Superiority dice add damage and a rider effect to your hits, giving you meaningful decisions every round and the ability to control the battlefield without magic.

## Features

### Combat Superiority

*3rd level*

You learn three maneuvers of your choice and gain four superiority dice (d8s), which you regain on a short or long rest.

You learn two additional maneuvers at 7th, 10th, and 15th level, and you can replace one maneuver with another whenever you learn a new one. You gain a fifth superiority die at 7th level and a sixth at 15th. The dice become d10s at 10th level and d12s at 18th.

Some maneuvers require saving throws; the DC equals **8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier** (your choice).

### Maneuvers

*3rd level*

The full list of maneuvers available. Except where noted, you expend one superiority die to use a maneuver.

| Maneuver | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ambush** *(TCE)* | Add the die to a Stealth check or initiative roll. |
| **Bait and Switch** *(TCE)* | Swap places with an ally within 5 feet; one of you gains the die as an AC bonus. |
| **Brace** *(TCE)* | Use your reaction to attack a creature that moves into your reach; add the die to damage. |
| **Commander's Strike** | Forgo one attack to let an ally use its reaction to attack, adding the die to damage. |
| **Commanding Presence** *(TCE)* | Add the die to an Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion check. |
| **Disarming Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; target makes a Strength save or drops an item. |
| **Distracting Strike** | On a hit, add the die to damage; the next attack against the target by another creature has advantage. |
| **Evasive Footwork** | Add the die to your AC while you move. |
| **Feinting Attack** | Bonus action to feint at a creature within 5 feet; gain advantage on your next attack against it and add the die to damage. |
| **Goading Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; target makes a Wisdom save or has disadvantage attacking anyone but you. |
| **Grappling Strike** *(TCE)* | After hitting, add the die to damage and make a grapple check with advantage. |
| **Lunging Attack** | Increase your reach by 5 feet for one attack and add the die to damage. |
| **Maneuvering Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; an ally can move half its speed without provoking from the target. |
| **Menacing Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; target makes a Wisdom save or is frightened until the end of your next turn. |
| **Parry** | Use your reaction to reduce damage from a melee attack by the die + your Dexterity modifier. |
| **Precision Attack** | Add the die to an attack roll — before or after rolling, but before the outcome is known. |
| **Pushing Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; target makes a Strength save or is pushed up to 15 feet. |
| **Quick Toss** *(TCE)* | Bonus action to draw and throw a weapon, adding the die to damage. |
| **Rally** | Bonus action to give an ally temporary hit points equal to the die + your Charisma modifier. |
| **Riposte** | When a creature misses you with a melee attack, use your reaction to attack it, adding the die to damage. |
| **Sweeping Attack** | On a hit, deal the die's damage to a second creature within 5 feet of the first. |
| **Tactical Assessment** *(TCE)* | Add the die to a History, Investigation, or Insight check. |
| **Trip Attack** | On a hit, add the die to damage; target makes a Strength save or is knocked prone. |

### Student of War

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

### Know Your Enemy

*7th level*

If you spend at least 1 minute observing or interacting with another creature outside combat, you can learn certain information about its capabilities compared to your own. The DM tells you if the creature is your equal, superior, or inferior in two of the following: Strength score, Dexterity score, Constitution score, AC, current hit points, total class levels, or fighter class levels.

### Improved Combat Superiority

*10th level*

Your superiority dice turn into d10s. At 18th level, they turn into d12s.

### Relentless

*15th level*

When you roll initiative and have no superiority dice remaining, you regain one superiority die.
