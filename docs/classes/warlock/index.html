Warlock

A pact-bound caster with few but always-maxed spell slots, refreshed on every short rest.

Source: Player's Handbook, p. 105

A warlock is defined by a pact with an otherworldly being. Sometimes the relationship is like that of a cleric and a deity, though the beings that serve as patrons for warlocks are not gods. More often, the arrangement is like that between a master and an apprentice — or a creditor and a debtor. The warlock gains power quickly and cheaply, and the price is rarely named up front.

Starting Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • a light crossbow and 20 bolts or any simple weapon
  • a component pouch or an arcane focus
  • a scholar's pack or a dungeoneer's pack
  • Leather armor
  • Any simple weapon
  • Two daggers

Alternatively, you may start with 4d4 x 10 gp and buy your own gear.

Multiclassing

Prerequisite: Charisma 13

Proficiencies gained: Light armor, Simple weapons

Spellcasting: Pact Magic slots are tracked separately from normal spell slots. You can use either type to cast spells you know from any class.

Pact Magic works differently from other spellcasting. You have very few slots — but they are always of the highest level you can cast, and you regain them all on a short rest, not just a long one. This makes the warlock the most sustainable caster over a long adventuring day.

Class Features

Otherworldly Patron

1st level

You have struck a bargain with an otherworldly being. Your choice grants you expanded spells and features at 1st, 6th, 10th, and 14th level.

Pact Magic

1st level

Your arcane research and the magic bestowed on you by your patron have given you facility with spells. See the Spell Slots and Slot Level columns — all your slots are the same level, and you regain them all when you finish a short or long rest.

Charisma is your spellcasting ability. You know two cantrips and two 1st-level spells at 1st level, and you can replace one known spell whenever you gain a warlock level.

Eldritch Invocations

2nd level

In your study of occult lore you have unearthed fragments of forbidden knowledge that imbue you with an abiding magical ability. You gain two invocations of your choice, and more as you level (see the table). Whenever you gain a warlock level, you can replace one invocation you know with another.

Some invocations have prerequisites — a minimum warlock level or a specific Pact Boon.

Pact Boon

3rd level

Your otherworldly patron bestows a gift upon you for your loyal service. Choose one.

Pact of the Chain. You learn find familiar and can cast it as a ritual. Your familiar can take additional forms — an imp, pseudodragon, quasit, or sprite. When you take the Attack action, you can forgo one of your own attacks to let your familiar make one attack with its reaction.

Pact of the Blade. You can use your action to create a pact weapon in your empty hand — any melee weapon you choose. You are proficient with it, it counts as magical, and it can be dismissed or transformed at will. You can also perform a 1-hour ritual to bond an existing magic weapon to yourself.

Pact of the Tome. Your patron gives you a Book of Shadows containing three cantrips of your choice from any class's spell list. They count as warlock cantrips for you and don't count against your cantrips known.

Pact of the Talisman (TCE). Your patron gives you an amulet. When the wearer fails an ability check, it can add a d4 to the roll, a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Ability Score Improvement

4th level

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

Eldritch Versatility

4th level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one cantrip you learned from this class's Pact Magic feature with another warlock cantrip, or change your choice of Pact Boon, or change the ability used by a feature. If the change would invalidate an invocation you know, you can also replace that invocation.

Mystic Arcanum

11th level

Your patron bestows upon you a magical secret called an arcanum. Choose one 6th-level spell from the warlock spell list as this arcanum. You can cast it once without expending a spell slot, regaining the ability on a long rest.

You gain a 7th-level arcanum at 13th level, an 8th-level at 15th, and a 9th-level at 17th.

Eldritch Master

20th level

You can draw on your inner reserve of mystical power while entreating your patron to regain expended spell slots. You can spend 1 minute entreating your patron for aid to regain all your expended Pact Magic spell slots. Once you regain slots with this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can do so again.
