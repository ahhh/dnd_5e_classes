# Path of the Storm Herald

*Barbarian Primal Path — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 10*

> Your rage projects an aura of desert heat, arctic cold, or crackling sea-storm.

Storm heralds learn to transform their rage into a mantle of primal magic that swirls around them. When in a fury, the barbarian taps into the forces of nature to create powerful magical effects centered on themselves.

**How it plays.** Turns rage into an automatic-damage or team-support aura. Sea does single-target lightning, Desert deals passive AoE, and Tundra hands out temporary hit points to the whole party every round.

## Features

### Storm Aura

*3rd level*

While raging, you emit a 10-foot-radius aura. Choose Desert, Sea, or Tundra when you gain this feature (you can change it whenever you gain a level in this class). The aura activates when you enter your rage, and you can activate its effect again as a bonus action on later turns.

**Desert.** Each other creature in your aura takes 2 fire damage. Increases at higher levels.

**Sea.** Choose one other creature in the aura; it must make a Dexterity save or take 1d6 lightning damage (half on a success).

**Tundra.** Each creature of your choice in the aura gains 2 temporary hit points.

### Storm Soul

*6th level*

You gain resistance and an environmental benefit matching your aura.

**Desert.** Fire resistance; you and up to ten friendly creatures are unharmed by temperatures as high as 120°F.

**Sea.** Lightning resistance; you can breathe underwater and gain a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

**Tundra.** Cold resistance; you can turn 5 cubic feet of water into ice, and you and up to ten friendly creatures are unharmed by temperatures as low as -50°F.

### Shielding Storm

*10th level*

Each creature of your choice within your Storm Aura has the damage resistance you gained from Storm Soul (fire, lightning, or cold).

### Raging Storm

*14th level*

Your aura gains an additional effect.

**Desert.** When a creature within the aura hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Dexterity save or take fire damage equal to half your barbarian level.

**Sea.** When you hit a creature in your aura with an attack, you can use your reaction to force it to make a Strength save or be knocked prone.

**Tundra.** Whenever the aura deals its temporary hit points, you can choose one creature affected by it; that creature must succeed on a Strength save or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of your next turn.
