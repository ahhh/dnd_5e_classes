Battle Master

Fighter Martial Archetype — Player's Handbook, p. 73

A tactical warrior who spends superiority dice on maneuvers that trip, disarm, and command.

Battle Masters employ martial techniques passed down through generations. To a Battle Master, combat is an academic field, sometimes an art form. They gather knowledge of maneuvers the way scholars gather lore.

How it plays. The most tactically rich martial subclass. Superiority dice add damage and a rider effect to your hits, giving you meaningful decisions every round and the ability to control the battlefield without magic.

Features

Combat Superiority

3rd level

You learn three maneuvers of your choice and gain four superiority dice (d8s), which you regain on a short or long rest.

You learn two additional maneuvers at 7th, 10th, and 15th level, and you can replace one maneuver with another whenever you learn a new one. You gain a fifth superiority die at 7th level and a sixth at 15th. The dice become d10s at 10th level and d12s at 18th.

Some maneuvers require saving throws; the DC equals 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Strength or Dexterity modifier (your choice).

Student of War

3rd level

You gain proficiency with one type of artisan's tools of your choice.

Know Your Enemy

7th level

If you spend at least 1 minute observing or interacting with another creature outside combat, you can learn certain information about its capabilities compared to your own. The DM tells you if the creature is your equal, superior, or inferior in two of the following: Strength score, Dexterity score, Constitution score, AC, current hit points, total class levels, or fighter class levels.

Improved Combat Superiority

10th level

Your superiority dice turn into d10s. At 18th level, they turn into d12s.

Relentless

15th level

When you roll initiative and have no superiority dice remaining, you regain one superiority die.
