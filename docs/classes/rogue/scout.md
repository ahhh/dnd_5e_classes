# Scout

*Rogue Roguish Archetype — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 47*

> A skirmisher and wilderness survivor who slips away whenever an enemy closes in.

You are skilled in stealth and surviving far from the streets of a city, allowing you to scout ahead of your companions during expeditions. Rogues who embrace this archetype are at home in the wilderness and among barbarians and rangers.

**How it plays.** A mobile, durable rogue with a ranger flavour. Skirmisher is free repositioning that keeps you out of melee, and Sudden Strike gives you a second Sneak Attack in the same round against a different target.

## Features

### Skirmisher

*3rd level*

You are difficult to pin down during a fight. You can move up to half your speed as a reaction when an enemy ends its turn within 5 feet of you. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

### Survivalist

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency in the Nature and Survival skills if you don't already have it. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of those proficiencies.

### Superior Mobility

*9th level*

Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. If you have a climbing or swimming speed, this increase applies to that speed as well.

### Ambush Master

*13th level*

You excel at leading ambushes and acting first in a fight. You have advantage on initiative rolls. In addition, the first creature you hit during the first round of a combat becomes easier for others to hit: attack rolls against that target have advantage until the start of your next turn.

### Sudden Strike

*17th level*

You can strike with deadly speed. If you take the Attack action on your turn, you can make one additional attack as a bonus action. This attack can benefit from your Sneak Attack even if you have already used it this turn, but you can't use Sneak Attack against the same target more than once in a turn.
