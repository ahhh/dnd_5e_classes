# Rune Knight

*Fighter Martial Archetype — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 42*

> A giant-taught warrior who carves magical runes into gear and grows to enormous size.

Rune Knights enhance their martial prowess using the supernatural power of runes, an ancient practice learned from giants. Rune magic was first found among the giants, and those who master it can call on the strength of the storm, the cunning of the fire giant, and the resilience of stone.

**How it plays.** One of the strongest and most flavourful fighter subclasses. Giant's Might grows you a size category with bonus damage and Strength advantage, and the runes give both combat and out-of-combat power.

## Features

### Bonus Proficiencies

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency with smith's tools, and you learn to speak, read, and write Giant.

### Rune Carver

*3rd level*

You can inscribe magical runes on your armor, weapons, or other gear. You learn two runes of your choice at 3rd level, and additional runes at 7th, 10th, and 15th level. Each rune can be invoked once per short or long rest.

| Rune | Passive Benefit | Invoke |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cloud** | Advantage on Sleight of Hand and Deception | Redirect an attack that hits you to another creature within 30 feet |
| **Fire** | Advantage on tool checks, +proficiency to a related check | On a hit, force a Strength save or the target takes fire damage and is restrained |
| **Frost** | Advantage on Animal Handling and Intimidation | Gain a bonus to Strength and Constitution checks and saves for 10 minutes |
| **Stone** | Advantage on Insight; darkvision 120 ft. | Force a creature within 30 feet to make a Wisdom save or be charmed for 1 minute |
| **Hill** *(7th+)* | Advantage on saves against being poisoned; poison resistance | Gain resistance to bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing damage for 1 minute |
| **Storm** *(10th+)* | Advantage on Arcana; can't be surprised while not incapacitated | For 1 minute, you can use a reaction to give an attacker disadvantage or an ally advantage |

### Giant's Might

*3rd level*

As a bonus action, you magically gain the following benefits for 1 minute: if you are smaller than Large, you become Large along with anything you're wearing; you have advantage on Strength checks and saving throws; and once on each of your turns, one of your attacks deals an extra 1d6 damage. The bonus damage increases to 1d8 at 10th level and 1d10 at 18th. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Runic Shield

*7th level*

When another creature you can see within 60 feet is hit by an attack roll, you can use your reaction to force the attacker to reroll the d20 and use the new roll. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Great Stature

*10th level*

The magic of your runes causes you to grow: roll 3d4, and you grow that number of inches permanently. Your Giant's Might bonus damage die also increases to 1d8.

### Master of Runes

*15th level*

You can invoke each rune you know twice, rather than once, regaining all uses on a short or long rest.

### Runic Juggernaut

*18th level*

Your Giant's Might extra damage increases to 1d10, and you can now become Huge, with your reach increasing by 5 feet.
