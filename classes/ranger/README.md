# Ranger

> A wilderness hunter blending martial skill, tracking expertise, and primal magic.

**d10 hit die** · **Dexterity, Wisdom** · Half Caster, Wisdom · 8 ranger archetypes · *Player's Handbook, p. 89*

Far from the bustle of cities and towns, past the hedges that shelter the most distant farms from the terrors of the wild, amid the dense-packed trees of trackless forests and across the wide-open plains, rangers keep their unending watch. They are hunters, guides, and guardians of the borderlands — equally at home stalking a monster through the deep woods and reading the signs of a coming storm.

## Build a Ranger

Work down this list to make a 1st-level ranger; the decision table at the end covers every later level.

**1. Abilities.** Put your highest score in **Dexterity, Wisdom** — it also sets your spell save DC and attack bonus. Your saving throw proficiencies are **Strength, Dexterity**.

**2. Hit points.** d10 hit die per level. At 1st level you have 10 + your Constitution modifier, and gain 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier on each level after.

**3. Proficiencies.**

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Armor | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields |
| Weapons | Simple weapons, Martial weapons |
| Tools | None |
| Skills | Choose 3 from Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception, Stealth, Survival |

**4. Equipment.** You start with the following, plus whatever your background grants:

- scale mail **or** leather armor
- two shortswords **or** two simple melee weapons
- a dungeoneer's pack **or** an explorer's pack
- A longbow and a quiver of 20 arrows

Or skip all of it and start with **5d4 x 10 gp** to buy your own gear.

**5. Spellcasting.** You cast with **Wisdom**, and your spells are **known** — a fixed list you swap on level-up. See [Spellcasting](#spellcasting) for the full block.

**6. Ranger Archetype.** Chosen at **3rd level** — browse the 8 options in [Ranger Archetypes](#ranger-archetypes) before you build, since it shapes the character more than anything else here.

### Multiclassing

- **Prerequisite:** Dexterity 13 and Wisdom 13

- **Spellcasting:** Add half your ranger level (rounded down) when determining shared multiclass spell slots.

## Class Features

### Favored Enemy

*1st level*

You have significant experience studying, tracking, hunting, and even talking to a certain type of enemy. Choose a favored enemy: aberrations, beasts, celestials, constructs, dragons, elementals, fey, fiends, giants, monstrosities, oozes, plants, or undead. Alternatively, you can select two races of humanoid.

You have advantage on Survival checks to track your favored enemies, as well as on Intelligence checks to recall information about them. You also learn one language spoken by your favored enemy. You choose an additional favored enemy at 6th and 14th level.

### Favored Foe

*1st level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

When you hit a creature with an attack roll, you can call on your mystical bond with nature to mark the target as your favored enemy for 1 minute (concentration). The first time on each of your turns you hit the favored enemy and deal damage, you increase that damage by 1d4. The die increases to 1d6 at 6th level and 1d8 at 14th. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Natural Explorer

*1st level*

You are a master of navigating the natural world. Choose one type of favored terrain: arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, or the Underdark. When you make an Intelligence or Wisdom check related to your favored terrain, your proficiency bonus is doubled if you are using a skill you're proficient in.

While travelling for an hour or more in your favored terrain, you gain these benefits: difficult terrain doesn't slow your group; your group can't become lost except by magic; you remain alert to danger even when foraging, navigating, or tracking; you can move stealthily alone at a normal pace; you find twice as much food when foraging; and you learn the exact number, size, and how long ago creatures passed through while tracking. You choose additional favored terrain at 6th and 10th level.

### Deft Explorer

*1st level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

You gain a series of benefits as you level, rather than terrain-specific ones.

**Canny (1st).** Choose one skill you're proficient in and gain expertise (double proficiency) with it. You also learn two languages.

**Roving (6th).** Your walking speed increases by 5 feet, and you gain a climbing speed and a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

**Tireless (10th).** As an action, you can give yourself temporary hit points equal to 1d8 + your Wisdom modifier, a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest. Whenever you finish a short rest, your exhaustion level (if any) is decreased by 1.

### Fighting Style

*2nd level*

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. See the Fighting Styles table above for the options available to rangers.

### Spellcasting

*2nd level*

You have learned to use the magical essence of nature to cast spells, much as a druid does. You know two ranger spells at 2nd level and learn more as you level up (see the Spells Known column). Wisdom is your spellcasting ability.

### Ranger Archetype

*3rd level*

You choose an archetype that you strive to emulate. It grants features at 3rd, 7th, 11th, and 15th level.

### Primeval Awareness

*3rd level*

You can use your action and expend one ranger spell slot to focus your awareness on the region around you. For 1 minute per level of the slot expended, you sense whether aberrations, celestials, dragons, elementals, fey, fiends, or undead are present within 1 mile (or 6 miles in your favored terrain). This doesn't reveal location or number.

### Primal Awareness

*3rd level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

You can focus your awareness through the interconnections of nature. You always have certain spells prepared, and you can cast each once per long rest without expending a slot: *speak with animals* (3rd), *beast sense* (5th), *speak with plants* (9th), *locate creature* (13th), and *commune with nature* (17th). They don't count against your spells known.

### Ability Score Improvement

*4th level*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

### Martial Versatility

*4th level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a fighting style you know with another fighting style available to rangers.

### Extra Attack

*5th level*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn.

### Land's Stride

*8th level*

Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement. You can also pass through nonmagical plants without being slowed and without taking damage from thorns, spines, or similar hazards. In addition, you have advantage on saving throws against plants that are magically created or manipulated to impede movement.

### Hide in Plain Sight

*10th level*

You can spend 1 minute creating camouflage for yourself, using fresh materials. Once camouflaged, you can try to hide by pressing yourself against a solid surface that is at least as tall and wide as you. You gain a +10 bonus to Stealth checks as long as you remain there without moving or taking actions.

### Nature's Veil

*10th level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

You can draw on the powers of nature to hide yourself. As a bonus action, you can magically become invisible, along with any equipment you carry, until the start of your next turn. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Vanish

*14th level*

You can use the Hide action as a bonus action on your turn. Also, you can't be tracked by nonmagical means, unless you choose to leave a trail.

### Feral Senses

*18th level*

You gain preternatural senses that help you fight creatures you can't see. When you attack a creature you can't see, your inability to see it doesn't impose disadvantage on your attack rolls. You are also aware of the location of any invisible creature within 30 feet, provided it isn't hidden from you and you aren't blinded or deafened.

### Foe Slayer

*20th level*

Once on each of your turns, you can add your Wisdom modifier to the attack roll or the damage roll of an attack you make against one of your favored enemies. You can use this before or after the roll, but before the effects of the roll are determined.
