Way of the Four Elements

Monk Monastic Tradition — Player's Handbook, p. 80

An elemental bender who spends ki to hurl fire, ride the wind, and shape stone.

You follow a monastic tradition that teaches you to harness the elements. When you focus your ki, you can align yourself with the forces of creation and bend the four elements to your will, using them as an extension of your body.

How it plays. The most flavourful monk and the weakest mechanically — disciplines are expensive in ki and the save DCs lag behind dedicated casters. Tasha's suggested fix is to let you swap disciplines when you level up.

Features

Disciple of the Elements

3rd level

You learn magical disciplines that harness the power of the four elements. You learn one discipline at 3rd level and one additional discipline at 6th, 11th, and 17th level.

Whenever you learn a new discipline, you can replace one discipline you already know with a different one. Casting a spell through a discipline costs ki points equal to the spell's level, plus additional ki as noted; you can spend additional ki to cast at a higher level, limited by your monk level (max 3 ki at 5th level, 4 at 9th, 5 at 13th, 6 at 17th).
