Oath of Glory

Paladin Sacred Oath — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 51

A mythic hero in the making — athletic feats, party-wide speed, and inspiring might.

Paladins who take the Oath of Glory believe they and their companions are destined to achieve glory through deeds of heroism. They train diligently and encourage their companions, so that all might reach their potential. Originally published in Mythic Odysseys of Theros.

How it plays. A strong support-and-mobility oath. Peerless Athlete makes you the party's best climber and jumper, Aura of Alacrity gives everyone +10 speed, and Glorious Defense is a superb reaction.

Features

Tenets of Glory

3rd level

Actions Over Words. Strive to be known by glorious deeds, not words. Challenges Are but Tests. Face hardships with courage, and encourage your allies to face them with you. Hone the Craft. Uphold the highest standards of training and preparation, so you are always ready for the challenge ahead. Discipline the Soul. Practice discipline of body, mind, and spirit.

Channel Divinity: Peerless Athlete

3rd level

As a bonus action, you gain advantage on Athletics and Acrobatics checks for 10 minutes. During that time, your carrying capacity doubles, and the distance you can jump increases by 10 feet.

Channel Divinity: Inspiring Smite

3rd level

Immediately after you deal damage to a creature with your Divine Smite feature, you can use Channel Divinity as a bonus action to distribute temporary hit points to creatures of your choice within 30 feet (which can include you). The total is 2d8 + your paladin level, divided as you choose.

Aura of Alacrity

7th level

Your walking speed increases by 10 feet. In addition, whenever an ally enters the aura for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, that ally's walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of its next turn. The aura is 5 feet in radius, increasing to 30 feet at 18th level.

Glorious Defense

15th level

When you or another creature you can see within 10 feet is hit by an attack roll, you can use your reaction to grant a bonus to the target's AC against that attack equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum +1), potentially causing it to miss. If the attack misses, you can make one weapon attack against the attacker as part of the same reaction, provided it is within your reach. You can use this a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier per long rest.

Living Legend

20th level

As a bonus action, you become supernaturally charismatic for 1 minute, gaining: advantage on all Charisma checks; once on each of your turns when you miss with a weapon attack, you can cause it to hit instead; and if you fail a saving throw, you can use your reaction to reroll it, taking the new result. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest, unless you expend a 5th-level spell slot.
