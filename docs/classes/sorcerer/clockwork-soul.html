Clockwork Soul

Sorcerer Sorcerous Origin — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 68

Mechanus order flows through you, cancelling advantage and disadvantage and warding allies.

The cosmic force of order has suffused you with magic. That power comes from Mechanus or a realm like it — a plane of clockwork precision. You might have consorted with a modron, entered a portal to Mechanus, or been touched by the plane's absolute regularity.

How it plays. A defensive support origin with excellent action economy. Restore Balance shuts down enemy advantage as a reaction, and Bastion of Law hands out a pool of damage-reduction dice to any ally.

Features

Clockwork Magic

1st level

You learn additional spells as you gain sorcerer levels (see the expanded list above). Each counts as a sorcerer spell for you but doesn't count against your number of spells known.

Whenever you gain a sorcerer level, you can replace one spell you gained from this feature with another spell of the same level from the abjuration or transmutation school.

Restore Balance

1st level

Your connection to the plane of absolute order allows you to equalize chaotic moments. When a creature you can see within 60 feet is about to roll a d20 with advantage or disadvantage, you can use your reaction to prevent the roll from being affected by advantage and disadvantage. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

Bastion of Law

6th level

You can tap into the grand equation of existence to imbue a creature with a shimmering shield of order. As an action, you can expend 1 to 5 sorcery points to create a magical ward around yourself or another creature you can see within 30 feet. The ward is represented by a number of d8s equal to the number of sorcery points spent, and lasts until you finish a long rest.

When the warded creature takes damage, it can expend any number of those dice, rolling them and reducing the damage taken by the total rolled.

Trance of Order

14th level

You gain the ability to enter a heightened state in which the cosmic forces of order guide you. As a bonus action, you can enter this state for 1 minute. During it, attack rolls against you can't benefit from advantage, and whenever you make an attack roll, an ability check, or a saving throw, you can treat a roll of 9 or lower on the d20 as a 10. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 5 sorcery points.

Clockwork Cavalcade

18th level

You summon spirits of order to expunge disorder around you. As an action, you summon the spirits in a 30-foot cube originating from you. They restore up to 100 hit points, divided as you choose among any number of creatures in the cube; end every spell of 6th level or lower on those creatures; and repair every damaged object in the cube. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest, unless you spend 7 sorcery points.
