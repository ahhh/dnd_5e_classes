# Way of the Drunken Master

**[Monk](../../README.md) Monastic Tradition** · *Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 33*

> A staggering, unpredictable brawler who lurches out of reach and redirects enemy attacks.

The Way of the Drunken Master teaches its students to move with the jerky, unpredictable movements of a drunkard. A drunken master sways, tottering on unsteady feet, to present what seems like an incompetent combatant who proves frustrating to engage.

**How it plays.** The mobility monk. Drunkard's Luck cancels disadvantage cheaply, Tipsy Sway gives you free repositioning after every Flurry, and Intoxicated Frenzy turns Flurry of Blows into a five-attack sweep.

Taken at **3rd level** as your Monastic Tradition. It gives you features at **3rd, 6th, 11th, 17th level**:

## Features

### Bonus Proficiencies

*3rd level*

You gain proficiency in the Performance skill if you don't already have it. You also gain proficiency with brewer's supplies.

### Drunken Technique

*3rd level*

Whenever you use Flurry of Blows, you gain the benefit of the Disengage action, and your walking speed increases by 10 feet until the end of the current turn.

### Tipsy Sway

*6th level*

You gain two benefits.

**Leap to Your Feet.** When you're prone, you can stand up by spending 5 feet of movement rather than half your speed.

**Redirect Attack.** When a creature misses you with a melee attack roll, you can spend 1 ki point as a reaction to cause that attack to hit one creature of your choice, other than the attacker, that you can see within 5 feet of you.

### Drunkard's Luck

*11th level*

Whenever you make an ability check, attack roll, or saving throw and have disadvantage, you can spend 2 ki points to cancel the disadvantage for that roll.

### Intoxicated Frenzy

*17th level*

When you use Flurry of Blows, you can make up to three additional attacks with it (up to a total of five), provided each attack targets a different creature.
