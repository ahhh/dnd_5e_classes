Cleric

A divine agent who channels a god's power to heal, smite, and turn back the undead.

Source: Player's Handbook, p. 56

Clerics are conduits for divine power. They are not merely priests — they are champions who wield the authority of their deity in the world, mending wounds, shattering the undead, and calling down miracles. The domain a cleric serves shapes them far more than the class itself, making the cleric the most varied class in the game.

Starting Equipment

You start with the following equipment, in addition to the equipment granted by your background:

  • a mace or a warhammer (if proficient)
  • scale mail or leather armor or chain mail (if proficient)
  • a light crossbow and 20 bolts or any simple weapon
  • a priest's pack or an explorer's pack
  • A shield
  • A holy symbol

Alternatively, you may start with 5d4 x 10 gp and buy your own gear.

Class Features

Spellcasting

1st level

As a conduit for divine power, you can cast cleric spells. You know three cantrips and prepare a number of spells each day equal to your Wisdom modifier + your cleric level. You use a holy symbol as a spellcasting focus, and can cast any prepared cleric spell with the ritual tag as a ritual.

Divine Domain

1st level

Choose a domain related to your deity. Your domain grants you bonus spells (always prepared, and not counted against your prepared total), a Channel Divinity option, and features at 1st, 2nd, 6th, 8th, and 17th level.

Channel Divinity

2nd level

You can channel divine energy directly from your deity to fuel magical effects. You start with two such effects — Turn Undead and one granted by your domain. You must finish a short or long rest to use it again. You gain a second use at 6th level and a third at 18th.

Some Channel Divinity effects require saving throws; the DC equals your cleric spell save DC.

Channel Divinity: Turn Undead

2nd level

As an action, you present your holy symbol and speak a prayer censuring the undead. Each undead within 30 feet that can see or hear you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes any damage — it must spend its turns trying to move as far away from you as it can, and it can't willingly move within 30 feet of you or take reactions. For its action it can only Dash or try to escape an effect preventing movement.

Harness Divine Power

2nd level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

You can expend a use of Channel Divinity as a bonus action to regain one expended spell slot, of a level no higher than half your proficiency bonus (rounded up). You can use this once per long rest, increasing to twice at 6th level and three times at 18th.

Ability Score Improvement

4th level

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

Cantrip Versatility

4th level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace one cantrip you learned from this class's Spellcasting feature with another cleric cantrip.

Destroy Undead

5th level

When an undead of CR 1/2 or lower fails its saving throw against your Turn Undead, it is instantly destroyed. The threshold rises to CR 1 at 8th level, CR 2 at 11th, CR 3 at 14th, and CR 4 at 17th.

Blessed Strikes

8th level Optional class feature · Tasha's Cauldron of Everything

Once per turn, when you hit a creature with a weapon attack or deal damage with a cantrip, you can deal an extra 1d8 radiant damage. This replaces the Divine Strike or Potent Spellcasting feature your domain would grant at 8th level.

Divine Intervention

10th level

As an action, you can call on your deity to intervene on your behalf. Describe the assistance you seek, then roll percentile dice. If you roll a number equal to or lower than your cleric level, your deity intervenes — the DM chooses the nature of the intervention, though the effect of any cleric spell or domain spell is appropriate.

If your deity intervenes, you can't use this feature again for 7 days; otherwise you can use it again after a long rest. At 20th level, your call for intervention succeeds automatically, with no roll required.
