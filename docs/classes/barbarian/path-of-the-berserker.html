Path of the Berserker

Barbarian Primal Path — Player's Handbook, p. 49

Unrestrained fury traded for exhaustion — the purest expression of rage as a weapon.

For berserkers, rage is a path to untrammeled fury. Slipping into rage means courting the bloodlust that lives in every barbarian's heart, letting it burn away caution, memory, and mercy alike.

How it plays. Simple and aggressive. Frenzy gives you a bonus-action attack every round of a rage, at the cost of a level of exhaustion afterward — best in short adventuring days or with a way to remove exhaustion.

Features

Frenzy

3rd level

When you rage, you can choose to go into a frenzy. For the duration of the rage you can make a single melee weapon attack as a bonus action on each of your turns after this one. When the rage ends, you suffer one level of exhaustion.

Mindless Rage

6th level

You cannot be charmed or frightened while raging. If you are charmed or frightened when you enter your rage, the effect is suspended for the duration.

Intimidating Presence

10th level

As an action, you can frighten a creature within 30 feet that can see and hear you. It must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw (DC 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier) or be frightened of you until the end of your next turn. On subsequent turns you can use your action to extend the effect. The creature is immune for 24 hours after succeeding on the save.

Retaliation

14th level

When you take damage from a creature within 5 feet of you, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature.
