# Artificer

> An inventor who infuses mundane objects with magic and treats items as spells.

**d8 hit die** · **Intelligence** · Half Caster, Intelligence — starts at 1st level · 4 artificer specialists · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 9*

Masters of invention, artificers use ingenuity and magic to unlock extraordinary capabilities in objects. They see magic as a complex system waiting to be decoded and harnessed — expressed in whirling gears, alchemical reactions, and infused gear. Artificers are the only class that creates permanent magic items as a core feature, and the only Intelligence-based half caster. First published in *Eberron: Rising from the Last War*, then revised in *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*.

## Build a Artificer

Work down this list to make a 1st-level artificer; the decision table at the end covers every later level.

**1. Abilities.** Put your highest score in **Intelligence** — it also sets your spell save DC and attack bonus. Your saving throw proficiencies are **Constitution, Intelligence**.

**2. Hit points.** d8 hit die per level. At 1st level you have 8 + your Constitution modifier, and gain 1d8 (or 5) + your Constitution modifier on each level after.

**3. Proficiencies.**

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Armor | Light armor, Medium armor, Shields |
| Weapons | Simple weapons |
| Tools | Thieves' tools, Tinker's tools, One type of artisan's tools of your choice |
| Skills | Choose 2 from Arcana, History, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Sleight of Hand |

**4. Equipment.** You start with the following, plus whatever your background grants:

- any two simple weapons
- a light crossbow and 20 bolts
- studded leather armor **or** scale mail
- Thieves' tools
- A dungeoneer's pack

Or skip all of it and start with **5d4 x 10 gp** to buy your own gear.

**5. Spellcasting.** You cast with **Intelligence**, and your spells are **prepared** — rebuilt after each long rest. You prepare Intelligence modifier + half your artificer level, rounded down (minimum 1). See [Spellcasting](#spellcasting) for the full block.

**6. Artificer Specialist.** Chosen at **3rd level** — browse the 4 options in [Artificer Specialists](#artificer-specialists) before you build, since it shapes the character more than anything else here.

### Multiclassing

- **Prerequisite:** Intelligence 13
- **You gain:** Light armor, Medium armor, Shields, Thieves' tools, Tinker's tools
- **Spellcasting:** Add half your artificer level, rounded up, when determining shared multiclass spell slots — the artificer rounds up where other half casters round down.

## Class Features

### Magical Tinkering

*1st level*

You have learned how to invest a spark of magic into mundane objects. At the end of a long rest — or with 1 minute of work — you can touch a Tiny nonmagical object and give it one of the following properties:

- It sheds bright light in a 5-foot radius and dim light for an additional 5 feet.
- Whenever tapped, it emits a recorded message up to 6 seconds long, audible within 10 feet.
- It continuously emits an odor or a nonverbal sound of your choice.
- A static visual effect appears on one of its surfaces.

You can have a number of objects affected equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum one).

### Spellcasting

*1st level*

You have studied the workings of magic and how to cast spells, channeling that magic through objects. You know two artificer cantrips and prepare a number of spells equal to your Intelligence modifier + half your artificer level (rounded down).

**Tools Required.** You produce your artificer spell effects through your tools. You must have a spellcasting focus — specifically thieves' tools or some kind of artisan's tool — in hand when you cast an artificer spell.

### Infuse Item

*2nd level*

You gain the ability to imbue mundane items with certain magical infusions, turning them into magic items.

Whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch a number of nonmagical objects equal to your Infused Items number and imbue each with one of your known infusions. An infusion works on only certain kinds of objects, as specified in its description. If the item requires attunement, you can attune to it the instant you infuse it.

You learn new infusions as you level (see the table), and whenever you gain an artificer level you can replace one infusion you know with another.

### Artificer Specialist

*3rd level*

You choose the type of specialist you are. It grants you features at 3rd, 5th, 9th, and 15th level, along with additional spells that are always prepared.

### The Right Tool for the Job

*3rd level*

You've learned how to produce exactly the tool you need. With thieves' tools or artisan's tools in hand, you can magically create one set of artisan's tools in an unoccupied space within 5 feet, over the course of 1 hour (which can be part of a short or long rest). The tools disappear after 1 hour or when you use this feature again.

### Ability Score Improvement

*4th level*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Gained again at 8th, 12th, 16th, and 19th level.

### Tool Expertise

*6th level*

Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses your proficiency with a tool.

### Flash of Genius

*7th level*

You gain the ability to come up with solutions under pressure. When you or another creature you can see within 30 feet makes an ability check or a saving throw, you can use your reaction to add your Intelligence modifier to the roll. You can use this a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once) per long rest.

### Magic Item Adept

*10th level*

You can attune to up to four magic items at once, rather than three. In addition, when you craft a common or uncommon magic item, it takes you a quarter of the normal time and costs you only half the usual gold.

### Spell-Storing Item

*11th level*

You can now store a spell in an object. Whenever you finish a long rest, you can touch one simple or martial weapon, or an item you can use as a spellcasting focus, and store a 1st- or 2nd-level artificer spell in it (one that has a casting time of 1 action).

A creature holding the item can use an action to cast the spell using your spellcasting ability modifier. The item can be used a number of times equal to twice your Intelligence modifier, then the spell ends.

### Magic Item Savant

*14th level*

You can attune to up to five magic items at once. In addition, you ignore all class, race, spell, and level requirements on attuning to or using a magic item.

### Magic Item Master

*18th level*

You can attune to up to six magic items at once.

### Soul of Artifice

*20th level*

You develop a mystical connection to your magic items, which you can draw on for protection. You gain a +1 bonus to all saving throws per magic item you are currently attuned to.

In addition, if you're reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can use your reaction to end one of your artificer infusions, causing you to drop to 1 hit point instead.
