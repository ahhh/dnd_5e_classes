# Oath of Vengeance

*Paladin Sacred Oath — Player's Handbook, p. 87*

> A relentless avenger who marks a single foe and hunts it down with haste and free attacks.

The Oath of Vengeance is a solemn commitment to punish those who have committed a grievous sin. Sometimes called avengers or dark knights, these paladins are willing to sacrifice even their own righteousness to mete out justice.

**How it plays.** The damage-focused oath, and the classic optimizer's choice. Vow of Enmity gives you advantage on every attack against one target, and *haste* and *hold monster* on your oath spell list are exceptional.

## Features

### Tenets of Vengeance

*3rd level*

**Fight the Greater Evil.** Faced with a choice of fighting my sworn foes or combating a lesser evil, I choose the greater evil.
**No Mercy for the Wicked.** Ordinary foes might win my mercy, but my sworn enemies do not.
**By Any Means Necessary.** My qualms can't get in the way of exterminating my foes.
**Restitution.** If my foes wreak ruin on the world, it is because I failed to stop them. I must help those harmed by their misdeeds.

### Channel Divinity: Abjure Enemy

*3rd level*

As an action, you choose one creature within 60 feet that you can see. That creature must make a Wisdom saving throw unless it is immune to being frightened. On a failure it is frightened and its speed is 0 for 1 minute; on a success, its speed is halved for 1 minute. The effect ends early if the creature takes damage.

### Channel Divinity: Vow of Enmity

*3rd level*

As a bonus action, you can utter a vow of enmity against a creature you can see within 10 feet. You gain advantage on attack rolls against it for 1 minute or until it drops to 0 hit points or falls unconscious.

### Relentless Avenger

*7th level*

When you hit a creature with an opportunity attack, you can move up to half your speed immediately after the attack and as part of the same reaction. This movement doesn't provoke opportunity attacks.

### Soul of Vengeance

*15th level*

When a creature under the effect of your Vow of Enmity makes an attack, you can use your reaction to make a melee weapon attack against that creature if it is within range.

### Avenging Angel

*20th level*

As an action, you can assume the form of an angelic avenger for 1 hour. You gain a flying speed of 60 feet, and you emanate an aura of menace in a 30-foot radius. The first time any enemy enters the aura or starts its turn there during a fight, it must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw or become frightened of you for 1 minute. While frightened, its speed is 0 and it takes psychic damage equal to half your level at the start of each of its turns. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest.
