# School of Abjuration

*Wizard Arcane Tradition — Player's Handbook, p. 115*

> A protective specialist wrapped in a self-recharging Arcane Ward that absorbs damage.

The School of Abjuration emphasizes magic that blocks, banishes, or protects. Detractors say the school is defensive and its tradition too focused on protection, but abjurers know that spells to ward, block, and banish are just as decisive as an evoker's fireball.

**How it plays.** The most durable wizard. Arcane Ward is a large pool of damage absorption that refills every time you cast an abjuration spell, and Spell Resistance at 14th makes you nearly immune to enemy magic.

## Features

### Abjuration Savant

*2nd level*

The gold and time you must spend to copy an abjuration spell into your spellbook is halved.

### Arcane Ward

*2nd level*

You can weave magic around yourself for protection. When you cast an abjuration spell of 1st level or higher, you can simultaneously use a strand of the spell's magic to create a magical ward on yourself that lasts until you finish a long rest. The ward has a hit point maximum equal to twice your wizard level + your Intelligence modifier.

Whenever you take damage, the ward takes the damage instead. If this reduces the ward to 0 hit points, you take any remaining damage. While the ward has 0 hit points, it can't absorb damage, but its magic remains — whenever you cast an abjuration spell of 1st level or higher, the ward regains hit points equal to twice the spell's level.

### Projected Ward

*6th level*

When a creature that you can see within 30 feet of you takes damage, you can use your reaction to cause your Arcane Ward to absorb that damage. If this reduces the ward to 0 hit points, the warded creature takes any remaining damage.

### Improved Abjuration

*10th level*

When you cast an abjuration spell that requires you to make an ability check as a part of casting it (as in *counterspell* and *dispel magic*), you add your proficiency bonus to that ability check.

### Spell Resistance

*14th level*

You have advantage on saving throws against spells. Furthermore, you have resistance against the damage of spells.
