# The Fathomless

*Warlock Otherworldly Patron — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 73*

> A kraken or deep entity grants you spectral tentacles and the crushing pressure of the abyss.

You have plumbed the depths of the multiverse's oceans and dark waters, making a bargain with a creature that dwells there — a kraken, a marid, an aboleth, or a stranger thing still. Your patron's power flows to you like a cold current from the deep.

**How it plays.** A solid, thematic patron with good action economy. Tentacle of the Deeps is a bonus-action attack you can use every turn for free, and Guardian Coil gives repeatable damage reduction.

## Features

### Tentacle of the Deeps

*1st level*

As a bonus action, you can magically summon a spectral tentacle at a point you can see within 60 feet. It lasts 1 minute, and you can move it up to 30 feet as a bonus action.

When you summon it and as a bonus action on later turns, you can make a melee spell attack against one creature within 10 feet of it. On a hit, the target takes 1d8 cold damage, and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn. The damage increases to 2d8 at 10th level. You can use this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Gift of the Sea

*1st level*

You gain a swimming speed of 40 feet, and you can breathe underwater.

### Oceanic Soul

*6th level*

You are now even more at home in the depths. You gain resistance to cold damage. In addition, when you are fully submerged, any creature that is also fully submerged can understand your speech, and you can understand theirs.

### Guardian Coil

*6th level*

Your Tentacle of the Deeps can defend you and others, interposing itself between them and danger. When you or a creature you can see takes damage while within 10 feet of the tentacle, you can use your reaction to reduce the damage by 1d8. The reduction increases to 2d8 at 10th level.

### Grasping Tentacles

*10th level*

You learn the spell *Evard's black tentacles*. It counts as a warlock spell for you but doesn't count against your spells known. You can cast it once without a spell slot, regaining that ability on a long rest.

Whenever you cast the spell, you gain temporary hit points equal to your warlock level, and if you take damage while concentrating on it, you have advantage on the Constitution save to maintain concentration.

### Fathomless Plunge

*14th level*

As an action, you can magically teleport yourself and up to five willing creatures within 30 feet to a body of water you have seen within 1 mile — or to a body of water on another plane, if your patron dwells there. You and the creatures appear in or next to that body of water. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a short or long rest.
