College of Lore

Bard Bard College — Player's Handbook, p. 54

Scholar-bards who collect secrets, cut enemies down with a word, and steal spells from every class.

Bards of the College of Lore know something about most things, collecting bits of knowledge from sources as diverse as scholarly tomes and peasant tales. Their motto is that no source of information is beneath notice.

How it plays. The strongest all-round bard. Cutting Words is a superb reaction debuff, and Additional Magical Secrets at 6th level gives you top-tier spells years ahead of schedule.

Features

Bonus Proficiencies

3rd level

You gain proficiency with three skills of your choice.

Cutting Words

3rd level

When a creature within 60 feet that you can see makes an attack roll, ability check, or damage roll, you can use your reaction to expend a Bardic Inspiration die and subtract the number rolled from the creature's total. You can do this after the roll but before you know whether it succeeds. A creature is unaffected if it can't hear you or is immune to being charmed.

Additional Magical Secrets

6th level

You learn two spells of your choice from any class. They count as bard spells for you but don't count against the number of bard spells you know.

Peerless Skill

14th level

When you make an ability check, you can expend one use of Bardic Inspiration and add the die roll to your total. You can do so after rolling but before knowing whether you succeed.
