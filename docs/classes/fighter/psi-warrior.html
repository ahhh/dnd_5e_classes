Psi Warrior

Fighter Martial Archetype — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 40

A psionic duelist who shields allies, hurls foes telekinetically, and eventually flies.

Psi Warriors augment their physical might with psi-infused weapon strikes, telekinetic lifts, and barriers of mental force. Some awaken this power through meditation; others are touched by aberrations or born with it.

How it plays. A flexible, resource-based fighter that scales with proficiency bonus rather than short rests. Protective Field is superb party mitigation, and Telekinetic Thrust adds free control to your attacks.

Features

Psionic Power

3rd level

You harbor a wellspring of psionic energy within yourself, represented by your Psionic Energy dice. You have a number of these dice equal to twice your proficiency bonus, and they start as d6s. You regain one die as a bonus action (once per short rest) and all of them on a long rest.

The die size increases as you level: d8 at 5th, d10 at 11th, and d12 at 17th.

Protective Field

3rd level

When you or another creature you can see within 30 feet takes damage, you can use your reaction to expend one Psionic Energy die, roll it, and reduce the damage by the number rolled + your Intelligence modifier (minimum 1).

Psionic Strike

3rd level

Once per turn, when you hit a target within 30 feet with a weapon attack, you can expend one Psionic Energy die to deal extra force damage equal to the die roll + your Intelligence modifier.

Telekinetic Movement

3rd level

You can move an object or a creature with your mind. As an action, choose one loose object that is Large or smaller, or one willing creature other than yourself, within 30 feet. You move it up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see. You can use this once per long rest without cost, or by expending a Psionic Energy die.

Telekinetic Adept

7th level

You gain two new abilities.

Psi-Powered Leap. As a bonus action, you gain a flying speed equal to twice your walking speed until the end of the turn. You can do this once per long rest without cost, or by expending a Psionic Energy die.

Telekinetic Thrust. When you deal damage with Psionic Strike, you can force the target to make a Strength saving throw (DC 8 + proficiency bonus + Intelligence modifier). On a failure, you knock it prone or move it up to 10 feet horizontally.

Guarded Mind

10th level

You have resistance to psychic damage. Moreover, if you start your turn charmed or frightened, you can expend one Psionic Energy die to end every effect on yourself subjecting you to those conditions.

Bulwark of Force

15th level

As a bonus action, you can choose creatures within 30 feet up to your Intelligence modifier (minimum one) — including yourself if you wish. Each gains half cover for 1 minute or until you're incapacitated. You can use this once per long rest without cost, or by expending a Psionic Energy die.

Telekinetic Master

18th level

You can cast the telekinesis spell, requiring no components and using Intelligence as your spellcasting ability. On each of your turns while concentrating on it, you can make one weapon attack as a bonus action. You can cast it once per long rest without a slot, or by expending a Psionic Energy die.
