Oath of the Watchers

Paladin Sacred Oath — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 53

A sentinel against extraplanar threats — initiative bonuses, spell defense, and banishment.

The Oath of the Watchers binds paladins to protect mortal realms from the predations of extraplanar creatures. Fiends, aberrations, and rogue celestials are their sworn enemies, and they train to counter the otherworldly magic such beings command.

How it plays. A specialist anti-caster and anti-planar oath, but its benefits are broadly useful. Aura of the Sentinel grants the whole party an initiative bonus, and Vigilant Rebuke punishes failed enemy saves.

Tenets of the Watchers

3rd level

Vigilance. The threats you face are cunning, powerful, and subtle. Be ever alert for their influence. Loyalty. Never accept gifts or favors from fiends or those who truck with them. Stand with your allies and hold them to the same standard. Discipline. Your body, mind, and soul must be disciplined to withstand the temptations and horrors of the multiverse.

Channel Divinity: Watcher's Will

3rd level

As an action, you can choose a number of creatures you can see within 30 feet, up to your Charisma modifier (minimum one). Each target has advantage on Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws for 1 minute.

Channel Divinity: Abjure the Extraplanar

3rd level

As an action, you present your holy symbol and each aberration, celestial, elemental, fey, or fiend within 30 feet that can hear you must make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure it is turned for 1 minute or until it takes damage.

Aura of the Sentinel

7th level

You emit an aura of alertness in a 10-foot radius while you aren't incapacitated. You and creatures of your choice in the aura gain a bonus to initiative rolls equal to your proficiency bonus. At 18th level, the range increases to 30 feet.

Vigilant Rebuke

15th level

Whenever you or a creature you can see within 30 feet succeeds on an Intelligence, Wisdom, or Charisma saving throw, you can use your reaction to deal 2d8 + your Charisma modifier force damage to the creature that forced the saving throw.

Mortal Bulwark

20th level

As a bonus action, you gain the following benefits for 1 minute: you gain truesight with a range of 120 feet; you have advantage on attack rolls against aberrations, celestials, elementals, fey, and fiends; and when you hit such a creature with an attack, it must succeed on a Charisma saving throw or be banished to its native plane (if it's not already there). Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest.
