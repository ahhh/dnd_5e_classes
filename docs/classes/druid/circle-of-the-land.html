Circle of the Land

Druid Druid Circle — Player's Handbook, p. 68

A mystic keeper of old lore whose chosen terrain grants extra spells and recovered slots.

The Circle of the Land is made up of mystics and sages who safeguard ancient knowledge and rites through a vast oral tradition. These druids meet in sacred circles of trees or standing stones to whisper primal secrets in Druidic.

How it plays. The caster-focused druid. Natural Recovery effectively gives you a second wind of spell slots every day, and your Circle Spells add wizard-style utility that the druid list otherwise lacks.

Features

Bonus Cantrip

2nd level

You learn one additional druid cantrip of your choice.

Natural Recovery

2nd level

During a short rest, you can recover expended spell slots with a combined level equal to or less than half your druid level (rounded up), none of which can be 6th level or higher. You can use this once per long rest.

Circle Spells

3rd level

Choose a type of land — arctic, coast, desert, forest, grassland, mountain, swamp, or Underdark. You gain the associated circle spells at 3rd, 5th, 7th, and 9th level (see the expanded spell list above). They are always prepared and don't count against the number of spells you can prepare.

Land's Stride

6th level

Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs you no extra movement, and you can pass through nonmagical plants without being slowed or taking damage from thorns and spines. You also have advantage on saving throws against plants that are magically created or manipulated to impede movement.

Nature's Ward

10th level

You can't be charmed or frightened by elementals or fey, and you are immune to poison and disease.

Nature's Sanctuary

14th level

When a beast or plant creature attacks you, it must make a Wisdom saving throw against your druid spell save DC. On a failure it must choose a different target, or the attack automatically misses. On a success it is immune to this effect for 24 hours.
