# Chronurgy Magic

*Wizard Arcane Tradition — Explorer's Guide to Wildemount, p. 185*

> A dunamancer of time who manipulates initiative, forces reroll results, and stores spells in a pearl.

Focusing on the manipulation of time, those who follow the Chronurgy tradition learn to alter the pace of reality. This dunamancy tradition is closely guarded by the Kryn Dynasty and the Cerberus Assembly, and its practitioners are among the most feared arcanists in Wildemount.

**How it plays.** Extremely powerful. Chronal Shift forces two rerolls per long rest on any roll you can see, Momentary Stasis removes a creature from a fight with no save-or-suck spell needed, and Convergent Future can simply declare a d20 result.

## Features

### Chronal Shift

*2nd level*

You can magically exert limited control over the flow of time around a creature. As a reaction, after you or a creature you can see within 30 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can force that creature to reroll. You make this decision after seeing whether the roll succeeds or fails, and the creature must use the new roll. You can use this feature twice, regaining all uses on a long rest.

### Temporal Awareness

*2nd level*

You can add your Intelligence modifier to your initiative rolls.

### Momentary Stasis

*6th level*

As an action, you can magically force a Large or smaller creature you can see within 60 feet to make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC. On a failure, the creature is encased in a field of magical energy until the end of your next turn or until it takes damage.

While encased this way, the creature is incapacitated and has a speed of 0. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Intelligence modifier (minimum once) per long rest.

### Arcane Abeyance

*10th level*

When you cast a spell using a spell slot of 4th level or lower, you can condense the spell's magic into a mote and store it in a pearl. As a bonus action, a creature holding the pearl can release the magic, casting the spell without using a spell slot or components, using your spellcasting ability modifier.

The pearl becomes nonmagical when the spell is cast or after 1 hour. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.

### Convergent Future

*14th level*

You can peer through possible futures and magically pull one into events around you. When you or a creature you can see within 60 feet makes an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can use your reaction to ignore the die roll and decide whether the number rolled is the minimum needed to succeed or one less than that number (your choice — a success or a failure).

When you use this feature, you gain one level of exhaustion. You can remove this exhaustion only with a long rest.
