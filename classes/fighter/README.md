# Fighter

> A master of weapons and armor who fights more often, more accurately, and longer than anyone.

**d10 hit die** · **Strength, Dexterity** · Non-caster · 10 martial archetypes · *Player's Handbook, p. 70*

Fighters share an unparalleled mastery with weapons and armor, and a thorough knowledge of the skills of combat. They are the most flexible martial class in the game — the archetype you pick can make you a rune-carved giant, a psychic duelist, an arcane spellblade, or simply the best pure warrior on the field.

## Build a Fighter

Work down this list to make a 1st-level fighter; the decision table at the end covers every later level.

**1. Abilities.** Put your highest score in **Strength, Dexterity**. Your saving throw proficiencies are **Strength, Constitution**.

**2. Hit points.** d10 hit die per level. At 1st level you have 10 + your Constitution modifier, and gain 1d10 (or 6) + your Constitution modifier on each level after.

**3. Proficiencies.**

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| Armor | All armor, Shields |
| Weapons | Simple weapons, Martial weapons |
| Tools | None |
| Skills | Choose 2 from Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation, Perception, Survival |

**4. Equipment.** You start with the following, plus whatever your background grants:

- chain mail **or** leather armor, longbow, and 20 arrows
- a martial weapon and a shield **or** two martial weapons
- a light crossbow and 20 bolts **or** two handaxes
- a dungeoneer's pack **or** an explorer's pack

Or skip all of it and start with **5d4 x 10 gp** to buy your own gear.

**5. Martial Archetype.** Chosen at **3rd level** — browse the 10 options in [Martial Archetypes](#martial-archetypes) before you build, since it shapes the character more than anything else here.

## Class Features

### Fighting Style

*1st level*

You adopt a particular style of fighting as your specialty. See the Fighting Styles table above for the options available to fighters.

### Second Wind

*1st level*

You have a limited well of stamina you can draw on to protect yourself from harm. As a bonus action, you can regain hit points equal to 1d10 + your fighter level. Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again.

### Action Surge

*2nd level*

You can push yourself beyond your normal limits for a moment. On your turn, you can take one additional action. Once you use this feature, you must finish a short or long rest before you can use it again. Starting at 17th level, you can use it twice before a rest, but only once on the same turn.

### Martial Archetype

*3rd level*

You choose an archetype that you strive to emulate in your combat styles and techniques. It grants features at 3rd, 7th, 10th, 15th, and 18th level.

### Ability Score Improvement

*4th level*

Increase one ability score by 2, or two ability scores by 1 each, to a maximum of 20. Fighters gain this feature more often than any other class: at 4th, 6th, 8th, 12th, 14th, 16th, and 19th level.

### Martial Versatility

*4th level* <sub>*Optional class feature* · *Tasha's Cauldron of Everything*</sub>

Whenever you gain an Ability Score Improvement, you can replace a fighting style you know with another fighting style available to fighters. If you're a Battle Master, you can also replace one maneuver you know with a different maneuver.

### Extra Attack

*5th level*

You can attack twice, instead of once, whenever you take the Attack action on your turn. The number increases to three attacks at 11th level and four attacks at 20th level — more than any other class in the game.

### Indomitable

*9th level*

You can reroll a saving throw that you fail, and must use the new roll. Once you use this feature, you must finish a long rest before you can use it again. You can use it twice between long rests starting at 13th level and three times starting at 17th level.
