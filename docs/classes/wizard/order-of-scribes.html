Order of Scribes

Wizard Arcane Tradition — Tasha's Cauldron of Everything, p. 78

Your spellbook wakes up, talks to you, and lets you swap damage types and copy spells free.

Magic of the Order of Scribes is drawn from the very act of writing. A wizard of this order awakens their spellbook, granting it a personality drawn from the many spellcasters whose work fills its pages. The book becomes a companion, a collaborator, and occasionally a critic.

How it plays. Extraordinary versatility. Awakened Spellbook lets you change a spell's damage type on the fly and cast a ritual once per day at no cost, and Master Scrivener creates free spell scrolls every long rest.

Features

Wizardly Quill

2nd level

As a bonus action, you can create a magic quill in your empty hand. It needs no ink, and when you write with it, it produces ink in any color you like. It vanishes if you create another quill or if you die.

The quill can erase any writing on a surface you touch as part of writing with it. When you use it to write in your spellbook, you can copy a spell into it in one minute per spell level, and you don't need to spend any money.

Awakened Spellbook

2nd level

Using specially prepared inks and mystic rituals, you have awakened an arcane sentience within your spellbook. It gains these properties:

  • You can use it as a spellcasting focus.
  • When you cast a wizard spell with a spell slot, you can temporarily replace its damage type with a type that appears in another spell in the book. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest.
  • When you cast a wizard spell as a ritual, you can use its normal casting time instead of adding 10 minutes. Once used, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest.

Manifest Mind

6th level

You can conjure forth the mind of your Awakened Spellbook. As a bonus action while the book is on your person, you can cause the mind to manifest as a Tiny spectral object hovering in an unoccupied space within 60 feet. It sheds light, is intangible, and can be moved 30 feet as a bonus action.

While it is present you can cast your wizard spells as though you were in the mind's space, and when you cast a spell that deals damage or restores hit points, you can have it originate from the mind. You can do this once per long rest for free, or by expending a spell slot of 1st level or higher.

Master Scrivener

10th level

Whenever you finish a long rest, you can create one magic scroll by touching your Wizardly Quill to a blank piece of paper and choosing one 1st- or 2nd-level spell that is in your spellbook and that requires 1 action to cast. The scroll takes shape and counts as a spell scroll.

The scroll disappears when you use this feature again. When you cast a spell from that scroll, add your Intelligence modifier to any damage or healing the spell deals.

One with the Word

14th level

Your connection to your Awakened Spellbook has become so profound that your soul has become entwined with it. While the book is on your person, you have advantage on all Arcana checks.

In addition, if you take damage that would reduce you to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage to 0 — expending a number of spell levels from the book equal to half your wizard level (rounded up) and erasing those spells from it. You then fall unconscious for 1d6 days unless magically healed. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest, and the erased spells must be recopied.
