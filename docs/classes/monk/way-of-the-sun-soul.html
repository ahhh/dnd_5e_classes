Way of the Sun Soul

Monk Monastic Tradition — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 34

A radiant warrior who fires bolts of searing light and detonates in a burst of flame.

Monks of the Way of the Sun Soul learn to channel their own life energy into searing bolts of light. They teach that meditation can unlock the ability to unleash the indestructible light of the soul itself. Originally published in the Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide.

How it plays. The ranged monk. Radiant Sun Bolt gives you a genuine ranged attack routine including bonus-action attacks, which no other monk gets — though its total damage lags behind melee traditions.

Features

Radiant Sun Bolt

3rd level

You can hurl searing bolts of magical radiance. You gain a new attack option that you can use with the Attack action: a ranged spell attack with a range of 30 feet, proficiency bonus and Dexterity modifier to the attack roll, dealing radiant damage equal to your Martial Arts die + your Dexterity modifier.

When you use the Attack action with this on your turn, you can spend 1 ki point to make two more bolt attacks as a bonus action.

Searing Arc Strike

6th level

Immediately after you take the Attack action on your turn, you can spend 2 ki points to cast burning hands as a bonus action. You can spend additional ki points to cast it at a higher level — one point per level above 1st, to a maximum equal to half your monk level.

Searing Sunburst

11th level

As an action, you create an orb of light at a point within 150 feet, which erupts into a 20-foot-radius sphere. Each creature in it must succeed on a Constitution saving throw or take 2d6 radiant damage. A creature doesn't need to make the save if it is behind total cover. You can increase the damage by 2d6 for each ki point spent, up to a maximum of 3 additional ki points.

Sun Shield

17th level

You become wreathed in a luminous aura, shedding bright light in a 30-foot radius. You can turn it on or off as a bonus action. If a creature hits you with a melee attack while the light shines, you can use your reaction to deal radiant damage to it equal to 5 + your Wisdom modifier.
