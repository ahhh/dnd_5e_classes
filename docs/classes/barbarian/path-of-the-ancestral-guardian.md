# Path of the Ancestral Guardian

*Barbarian Primal Path — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 9*

> Spirits of your ancestors shield your allies and punish those who ignore you.

Some barbarians hail from cultures that revere their ancestors. These warriors call on the spirits of the honored dead to guard them and their kin, hindering their foes and turning aside blows meant for their companions.

**How it plays.** The best defensive-support barbarian. Ancestral Protectors is a genuine 'taunt' — enemies you hit have disadvantage attacking anyone else — and Spirit Shield redirects damage away from allies.

## Features

### Ancestral Protectors

*3rd level*

While raging, the first creature you hit with an attack on your turn becomes wreathed in spectral warriors until the start of your next turn. The creature has disadvantage on attack rolls against anyone but you, and when it hits a target other than you, that target has resistance to the damage dealt.

### Spirit Shield

*6th level*

While raging, if a creature you can see within 30 feet takes damage, you can use your reaction to reduce that damage by 2d6. This increases to 3d6 at 10th level and 4d6 at 14th level.

### Consult the Spirits

*10th level*

You can cast *augury* or *clairvoyance* without a spell slot or material components, using Wisdom as your spellcasting ability. Your spirits perform the divination. Once used, you can't use this feature again until you finish a short or long rest.

### Vengeful Ancestors

*14th level*

When you use Spirit Shield to reduce damage from an attack, the attacker takes the damage you prevented — no attack roll or saving throw required.
