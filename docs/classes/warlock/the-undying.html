The Undying

Warlock Otherworldly Patron — Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide, p. 139

A pact with a deathless being who has cheated the grave and shares that endurance with you.

Your patron is a mortal who has, through some unnatural means, conquered death. Liches, vampires, and the deathless of Aerenal seek to unlock the secret of eternal life — and in exchange for your service, your patron shares a fragment of that unnatural persistence.

How it plays. A durability-focused patron, generally considered weaker than The Undead (which covers similar ground). Among the Dead is a solid anti-undead defense, and Indestructible Life provides bonus-action self-healing.

Features

Among the Dead

1st level

You learn the spare the dying cantrip, which doesn't count against your number of cantrips known. You also have advantage on saving throws against any disease.

Additionally, undead have difficulty harming you: if an undead targets you directly with an attack or a harmful spell, it must make a Wisdom saving throw against your spell save DC (it doesn't need to make the save for area effects). On a failure it must choose a new target or forfeit the attack, and on a success it is immune to this effect for 24 hours.

Defy Death

6th level

You can give yourself vitality when you cheat death or when you help someone else cheat it. You regain hit points equal to 1d8 + your Constitution modifier (minimum 1) when you succeed on a death saving throw, or when you stabilize a creature with spare the dying. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest.

Undying Nature

10th level

You can hold your breath indefinitely, and you don't require food, water, or sleep — although you still require rest to reduce exhaustion and must sleep to gain the benefit of a long rest. You also age at a slower rate: for every 10 years that pass, your body ages only 1 year. And you are immune to disease.

Indestructible Life

14th level

You partake of some of the true secrets of the undying. As a bonus action, you can regain hit points equal to 1d8 + your warlock level. Additionally, if you place a severed body part of yours back where it belongs, it reattaches instantly. You can use this feature once per short or long rest.
