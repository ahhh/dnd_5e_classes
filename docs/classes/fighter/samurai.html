Samurai

Fighter Martial Archetype — Xanathar's Guide to Everything, p. 31

An indomitable warrior whose fighting spirit grants advantage, temp HP, and a refusal to fall.

The Samurai is a fighter who draws on an implacable fighting spirit to overcome enemies. A samurai's resolve is nearly unbreakable, and the foes in a samurai's path have two choices: yield or die fighting.

How it plays. Excellent damage and survivability with almost no complexity. Fighting Spirit gives you advantage on all attacks for a turn plus temp HP, three times per rest — it pairs beautifully with Action Surge and Sharpshooter/Great Weapon Master.

Features

Bonus Proficiency

3rd level

You gain proficiency in one of the following skills: History, Insight, Performance, or Persuasion. Alternatively, you learn one language of your choice.

Fighting Spirit

3rd level

As a bonus action on your turn, you can give yourself advantage on all weapon attack rolls until the end of the current turn. When you do so, you also gain 5 temporary hit points. The temporary hit points increase to 10 at 10th level and 15 at 15th. You can use this feature three times per long rest.

Elegant Courtier

7th level

Your discipline and attention to detail allow you to excel in social situations. Whenever you make a Persuasion check, you gain a bonus equal to your Wisdom modifier. Your self-control also grants you proficiency in Wisdom saving throws — or if you already have it, you gain proficiency in Intelligence or Charisma saving throws (your choice).

Tireless Spirit

10th level

When you roll initiative and have no uses of Fighting Spirit remaining, you regain one use.

Rapid Strike

15th level

If you have advantage on a weapon attack roll against a target on your turn, you can forgo that advantage and immediately make an additional weapon attack against the same target as part of the same action. You can do this only once per turn.

Strength before Death

18th level

When you take damage that would reduce you to 0 hit points, you can use your reaction to delay falling unconscious and immediately take an extra turn — resolving it right after your reaction. If you still have 0 hit points at the end of that turn, you fall unconscious as normal. Once used, you can't use this again until you finish a long rest.
